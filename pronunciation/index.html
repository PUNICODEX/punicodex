The Doctrine of Sound

The Pronunciation Standard

AI voices flatten the gods into English reading rules. This is the mechanical system that says each name the way it was meant to be said — the same marks we restore, decoded into sound and time.

The Meta-Rule

The marks are the pronunciation

Every restored name on PuniCodex carries its own speaking instructions. Three marks do all the work — and they are read mechanically, never by editorial mood.

Acute ( á )

Stress this syllable. Zeús is ZE-oos, never "zoose". The accent is where the voice rises.

Macron ( ā )

Hold the vowel double — one extra beat of time. Apóllōn holds the final ō twice as long as the rest.

Circumflex ( â )

Stress and length in one mark, with a falling pitch contour across the long syllable — Athēnâ's final vowel.

The Timing Model

Names are measured in morae

A name is not a string of sounds but a string of time. Each syllable earns morae — the unit classical Greek, Japanese, and Sanskrit all independently use:

ShapeMoraeExample
Short vowel1a in Apóllōn — a flicker of time
Long vowel / diphthong2ō in Apóllōn — double the hold
Geminate consonant+1ll in Apóllōn — the held liquid
Heavy coda+0.5closed syllable tails, rounded per syllable

Read our beat strings as: ˘ = one mora, ¯ = two, ¯˘ = three. Apóllōn = ˘ ¯ ¯˘ — 1, 2, 3 morae, ~660 ms, stress rising on the middle syllable.

Egyptian Conventional

Hieroglyphs write consonants, not vowels. Egyptological readings (Rꜥ as "Ra", Ḥr as "Hor") are conventions of the field, not derivations — and they are labelled "Conventional reading" wherever they appear. We do not fake certainty the script doesn't offer.

For Machines

Speakable by contract

Every name in the lexicon ships a machine-ready pronunciation record:

API

GET /api/v1/names/{id}/pronunciation — IPA, respelling, syllables, stress, SSML markup, and the full timing object (morae, beats, contour, durations).

SSML

Each record carries <phoneme alphabet="ipa"> payloads that ElevenLabs, Google TTS, Amazon Polly, and Azure accept natively.

Corpus

data/corpus/pronunciation.jsonl — 900+ records for training and evaluation, refreshed with every dataset generation.

The Honest Boundary

Derived, conventional, or curated

Most flagship names are derived mechanically from their restored marks (and validated against the curated pronunciation atlas — currently ~82% exact-or-near across 893 entries, 89% mora agreement on flagships). Egyptian readings are labelled conventional. Everything else falls back to the curated atlas rather than a guessed rule. Where the rules end, we say so.
