University Sponsorship

PuniCodex restores the original Unicode names of the gods to the digital realm. Founding academic collaborators put their institution's name on that work — and their students behind it.

Apply for Sponsorship → Email the Partnerships Team

What a Founding Collaborator Receives

One annual sponsorship, four concrete deliverables for your department:

  • I.

    Sponsorship Strip Placement

    Your institution's name, monogram or logo, and link appear in the Academic Collaborators strip rendered across the public site — the homepage and every temple page — with a Founding Partner tier label.

  • II.

    Scholarly Edition Mentoring

    Provisioned student accounts let your students research and publish peer-reviewed sections on flagship temples under the Scholarly Edition's editorial quality gate — public, citable work with faculty mentorship credited.

  • III.

    Creative Marketplace

    Student artwork and design materials are listed — watermarked — in the Creatives marketplace, where sponsors and licensees can commission or license them.

  • IV.

    Department Analytics

    An institution dashboard shows what your students have contributed, review status, engagement, and the reach of your department's published work.

How It Works

  1. Apply. Submit the university sponsorship application; a curator reviews it and arranges an introductory call.
  2. Provision. On approval we create your institution and administrator account; your admin then provisions student and faculty credentials.
  3. Contribute. Scholars publish through the editorial quality gate; Creatives upload watermarked work to the marketplace.
  4. Be seen. Your institution appears in the collaborators strip and in the credits of every published contribution.

Who Should Partner

Classics, Ancient History, Religious Studies, Linguistics, Digital Humanities, Design, and Marketing departments that want to give students real-world, publishable experience on a public scholarly platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does university sponsorship cost?

Founding collaborator sponsorship starts at $999 per year per institution. Custom enterprise packages for multiple departments or campuses are available on request.

How does our institution apply?

Submit the university sponsorship application. A curator reviews every application, arranges an introductory call, and on approval provisions your institution and its administrator account with a one-time temporary password.

How do our students get access?

Your institution's administrator provisions and revokes student credentials directly. Student work passes through the Scholarly Edition quality gate before publication, and access can be revoked at any time with immediate effect.

Where does our institution's name appear?

In the Academic Collaborators strip rendered across the public site — the homepage and every temple page — with your name, monogram or logo, link, and tier label, and in the credits of the Scholarly Edition sections your students publish.

Who do we speak to before applying?

Email support@punicodex.com with the subject University Collaborator Sponsorship. The partnerships team typically replies within one business day.
