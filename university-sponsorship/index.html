Three instruments, one collaboration

Everything below is included from the first day — there is no paid tier that unlocks the good parts.

I — THE EDITION

The Scholarly Edition

Your students research and publish peer-reviewed sections on the flagship temples — mythology, archaeology, attestation, source criticism — under an editorial quality gate, public, bylined and citable.

Work passes review before it ships; faculty mentorship is credited; every published contribution carries your institution's name.

II — THE MARKETPLACE

The Creatives Marketplace

Student art inspired by a temple is listed — watermarked — and, once it clears review, printed on that temple's merchandise in the Reliquary store. Half of every sale is paid to the creator.

Income, not just credit: a design student can watch their work sell as a hoodie, a mug, a canvas — with their name on it.

III — THE ENGINE

The Pronunciation Engine

A rules-based engine derives IPA, respelling, syllables and stress for all 980 names, per pantheon — Attic Greek to Mandarin pinyin. Egyptian entries are honestly labelled "conventional reading" where the vowels are lost.

A teaching instrument your linguistics and classics classes can pull apart — with an open API and CC BY 4.0 datasets to cite.

Complimentary. Forever.

We provision your institution with an administrator account; your admin provisions and revokes student credentials directly. Work passes an editorial quality gate before publication, and access can be revoked at any time, with immediate effect.

What we ask in return is one thing: a link from your department's website to punicodex.com within your first year — features are live the moment you're provisioned; most departments add the link in their next site update, and we verify it automatically. Your institution's name also appears in the Academic Collaborators strip across the public site and in the credits of every contribution your students publish.

How It Works

  1. Begin. Apply below — or reply to our invitation. A curator reviews and arranges a fifteen-minute introductory call.
  2. Provision. We create your institution and administrator account; your admin provisions student and faculty credentials.
  3. Contribute. Scholars publish through the editorial gate; creatives upload to the marketplace; approved art goes on sale in the store.
  4. Be seen — and paid. Your institution is credited across the platform; your students earn royalties on every sale of their work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does academic collaboration cost?

Nothing. The Scholarly Edition, the Creatives Marketplace, the pronunciation engine and the API are complimentary for academic collaborators — there is no invoice and no renewal. In return we ask one thing: a backlink from your department's website to punicodex.com within your first year — everything starts immediately, and a gentle automated check follows up until the link lands. A separate, optional Founding Partner sponsorship tier (site-wide placement, from $999/year) exists for institutions that want it; the collaboration itself never depends on it.

How do our students get paid?

When a student's creative clears review and is printed on temple merchandise in the Reliquary store, 50% of every sale is paid to the creator. The marketplace handles the licensing, the watermarking and the royalty ledger.

Who controls access?

Your institution's administrator provisions and revokes student credentials directly. Student work passes an editorial quality gate before publication, and access can be revoked at any time, with immediate effect.

Where does our institution's name appear?

In the Academic Collaborators strip rendered across the public site — the homepage and every temple page — and in the credits of every Scholarly Edition section your students publish.

Who do we speak to first?

Email support@punicodex.com with the subject Academic Collaboration, or apply below. We typically reply within one business day.
