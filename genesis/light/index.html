The First Light

The sun was never merely a lamp.

To see the light was to know — and to be known.

Every culture hung its measure of time, truth, and kingship

on the same burning disc.

And when the disc set, mortals kept their own small suns —

hearth, altar, torch. Fire is the light we carry.
