Earth & Sky → The Vertical World

The great parting never stopped. We still build the split — upward into towers and orbit, downward into mines and foundations.

The separation as a profession: foundations, towers, and everything holding them apart. The sky, permanently occupied — the aviation industry sails what Ouranos once kept. Orbit is the new heaven: the final separation of earth from the sky-dweller. The measured middle ground — every property is a border between earth and sky. The deep claim on the earth’s body — modern geology, ancient Geb. The drawn sky — architecture is the separation, designed before it is built.
