The Wild

Between the cities of the gods and the roads of men

lay the old country — the forest that predates the name,

the hunt that feeds and the beast that teaches.

Civilisation is a clearing. The wild is what surrounds it.

The Wild → The Preserve

The wild was never conquered — it was zoned. Every park, reserve, and pet is the old country under new management.

The managed forest — Artemis still owns it; we just bill for the stewardship. The wolf at the hearth — the first domestication, still the largest. The pilgrimage to the wild — the safari is the hunt, reversed.
