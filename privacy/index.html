PRIVACY & TRUST

We do not log keystrokes. We do not collect typed text. Everything stays on your device.

The Promise

Zero Keylogging

PÚNYCODEX is a keyboard for scholarly Unicode names. We understand that trust is everything when it comes to input methods. We do not log, transmit, or store anything you type.

Your keystrokes are processed entirely on your device. The suggestion engine runs locally using a bundled lexicon. No text ever leaves your phone.

If we wouldn't trust a keyboard with our own passwords, we wouldn't ask you to trust ours.

What We Collect

Nothing You Type

The keyboard itself collects zero data about your typing. We have no analytics in the keyboard service. We do not use Firebase, Crashlytics, or any third-party SDK inside the keyboard.

The companion app (the PÚNYCODEX Type shell) stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Recent conversions — your last 50 typed name conversions, stored in local app storage
  • Favorites — names you have starred, stored in local app storage
  • Session stats — conversion counts and day streaks, stored in local app storage

None of this data is uploaded, synced, or transmitted. Clearing app storage removes it permanently.

Permissions

Why We Ask

The Android keyboard requires two permissions:

  • VIBRATE — for haptic key feedback (can be disabled in system settings)
  • INTERNET — used only by the companion app shell (not the keyboard service) to check domain availability and load the Unicode directory

The keyboard service itself does not use the internet. It operates entirely offline.

Third Parties

No Trackers

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or any tracking SDK. We do not serve ads. We do not sell data.

The only external service the companion app contacts is our own API at punycodex.com for domain status checks and the Unicode character directory.

Contact

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at hello@punycodex.com.
