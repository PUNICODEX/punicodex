TROUBLESHOOTING
===============================================================================

Q: I get a "domain not configured" error on Vercel
A: The A record hasn't propagated yet. Wait 10-30 minutes, or check
   https://vercel.com/dashboard and look for the domain status.

Q: Cloudflare shows "Invalid SSL Certificate"
A: You enabled the orange cloud (proxy). Toggle it to gray (unproxied) for
   Cloudflare Pages custom domains.

Q: GoDaddy doesn't have an "Import Zone File" option
A: Not all GoDaddy plans support this. Add records one by one using the
   standard DNS editor. It takes about 30 seconds per domain.

Q: Can I automate the Cloudflare DNS?
A: Yes! Update your API token at https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens
   to include "Zone:Edit" permission. Then re-run the deployment scripts.
