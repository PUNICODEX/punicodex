The load-bearing truth

Every layer is still holding.

Nothing in this excavation is dead weight. The days of the week are Norse. The alphabet is Phoenician. The flood is Sumerian. Yoga is Vedic, mindfulness is Buddhist, the obelisk is Egyptian, the map is Greek — and beneath them all, never once interrupted, the Dreamtime. The modern world is not built on the ruins of these strata. It is built of them.
