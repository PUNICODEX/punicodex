How to Play

✦ Ink

Ink is your only currency. Your maximum rises by one each round (up to 10) and refills every turn. Spend it to summon cards — and earn more of it in battle to buy packs.

⚔ Combat

Minions strike and counter-strike simultaneously. A minion cannot attack the turn it is played, and each minion attacks once per turn. Reduce the enemy hero from 30 HP to 0 to win.

≫ Speed

A faster attacker outmaneuvers its defender: the counter-attack is halved. Slow effects halve a minion's speed permanently — softening even the mightiest foe.

✧ Abilities

Battlecries fire when played, deathrattles when destroyed, passives while on board. Combo cards reward playing multiple cards in one turn. All 25 scholarly effect kinds are live.
