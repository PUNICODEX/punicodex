TITLE: Chapter VI. Of Making the Shabti to Work

The Chapter of making the shabti figure to do work for a man in the underworld. The scribe Nebseni, the draughtsman in the Temples of the North and South, the man highly venerated in the Temple of Ptah, saith:—

O thou shabti figure of the scribe Nebseni, the son of the scribe Thena, victorious, and of the lady of the house Mutrestha, victorious, if I be called, or if I be adjudged to do any work whatsoever of the labours which are to be done in the underworld—behold, [for thee] opposition will there be set aside—by a man in his turn, let the judgment fall upon thee instead of upon me always, in the matter of sowing the fields, of filling the water-courses with water, and of bringing the sands of this east [to] the west.

[The shabti figure answereth], "Verily I am here [and will come] whithersoever thou biddest me."
