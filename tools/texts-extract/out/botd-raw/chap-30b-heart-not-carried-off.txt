In some ancient papyri the text of this chapter is made to follow the Rubric of Chapter LXIYV., with which it had some close connexion, and in others it follows the Rubric of Chapter CXLVIII. The Rubrical direction concerning Chapter LXIV. reads :—" Behold, make a scarab of green stone, wash it with gold and place it in the heart of a man (i.e., the deceased), and it will perform for him the 'opening of the mouth'; anoint it with dutw unguent, and recite over it as a charm the following words :—' My heart, my mother ; my heart, my mother!'" ete. In the Turin Papyrus (Lepsius, T'odtenbuch, Bl. 16) it follows Chapter XXX. which contains parts of Chapters XX Xa. and XXXs.
