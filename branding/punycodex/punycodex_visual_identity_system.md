## Master Style Guide

### Art Direction
- **Style**: Epic anime/manga illustration with mythological grandeur
- **Proportions**: Adult heroic figures (NOT chibi/kawaii)
- **Rendering**: Detailed cel-shading with dramatic lighting
- **Poses**: Dynamic, full-body action or commanding stances
- **Background**: Transparent (PNG) for versatile placement
- **Aspect Ratio**: 2:3 portrait (mascot), 1:1 (logomark), 3:2 (logolockup)
- **Accent System**: Gold is the unifying accent across all 24 deities

### Universal Gold Accent
Every deity incorporates **#D4AF37** (Classic Gold) as a unifying accent color — appearing in armor trim, jewelry, crowns, weapons, or decorative elements. This creates visual cohesion across the entire Pantheon collection.
