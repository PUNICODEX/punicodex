Unicode Domain Appraiser

The first valuation model built for Unicode / IDN domain names. It values what a name means: restoration quality, scholarly attestation, development depth, and real-world industry demand from the PuniCodex pattern graph — the industries a name's meaning aligns with are its end-user market. Tenant revenue from live sponsorship inventory is reported separately. No factor in this engine reads, references, or derives value from any third-party brand; the folded ASCII twin is shown for market context only.

Estimates are automated model output — not professional valuations or financial advice. The Appraisal & Estimate Terms apply.
