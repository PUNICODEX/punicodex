Creative Marketplace Terms

PUNICODEX Student Creator Program

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

1. Definitions

"PUNICODEX" / "we" / "us"
PUNICODEX, the operator of the PUNICODEX website, the Creative Marketplace, and the Reliquary store.
"Marketplace"
The PUNICODEX Creative Marketplace, accessible at punicodex.com/creatives/, through which student artwork is exhibited, licensed, and sold as merchandise.
"Creator"
A verified student at a Sponsored Institution who uploads Works to the Marketplace.
"Work"
An original myth-inspired artwork uploaded by a Creator, including illustrations, paintings, digital art, and glyph compositions.
"Sponsored Institution"
A university or college with an active PUNICODEX institution sponsorship, whose departments administer Creator eligibility.
"Department Allowlist"
The register of eligible students maintained by a department of a Sponsored Institution. Only students on a Department Allowlist may hold verified Creator accounts.
"Merch Consent"
The Creator's explicit, opt-in grant of merchandise licensing rights to PUNICODEX for a specific Work, as described in Section 4.
"Net Margin"
For a given sale of a Product bearing a Work: the gross sale price actually received by PUNICODEX for that Product (excluding shipping charges and any taxes or duties collected), less the fulfilment partner's base production cost, less payment processing fees charged on the transaction.
"Creator Share"
Fifty percent (50%) of the Net Margin on each sale of a Product bearing the Creator's Work.
"Buyer"
A purchaser of a Digital License to a Work through the Marketplace.
"Digital License"
A paid license granting a Buyer access to the unwatermarked original file of a Work, subject to the restrictions in Section 7.
"Product"
A made-to-order physical good sold through the Reliquary (punicodex.com/store/) bearing a Work, governed by the Reliquary Sale Terms.

2. Eligibility & Accounts

2.1 Verified Student Accounts Only

The Marketplace is open exclusively to verified student accounts. Every Creator must hold an account whose student status has been verified through their Sponsored Institution before uploading Works.

2.2 Institution Sponsorship & Department Allowlist

Eligibility is governed by two institutional controls, both of which must be satisfied:

  • The Creator's university must hold an active institution sponsorship with PUNICODEX; and
  • The Creator must appear on a Department Allowlist administered by their department.

If a sponsorship lapses or a student is removed from the Department Allowlist (for example, on graduation or withdrawal), the affected Creator may no longer upload new Works. Works already listed remain governed by these Terms unless withdrawn under Section 4.3.

2.3 Age & Guardian Consent

Creators must be 18 years of age or older. Students under 18 may participate only with guardian consent verified by their Sponsored Institution, administered by the university through its own consent and enrolment processes. PUNICODEX relies on the institution's verification and does not independently collect guardian consent records.

3. Uploading Works & Moderation

3.1 Original Myth-Inspired Artwork

Creators may upload original, myth-inspired artwork. Works should engage with the mythological, linguistic, and pantheon themes of the PUNICODEX lexicon. Uploaded Works are exhibited in the Marketplace with attribution to the Creator and, where applicable, their institution and department.

3.2 Moderation & Blocklist

All Works pass through moderation before listing. PUNICODEX enforces a moderation blocklist covering, among other things, third-party trademarks, confusable brand imitations, and prohibited content. Works that trip the blocklist or fail review are not listed; repeated attempts to upload prohibited material may result in account suspension.

3.3 Delisting at Discretion

PUNICODEX may delist any Work at its discretion, including in response to third-party rights claims, blocklist updates, quality standards, or changes in institutional sponsorship status. Where practicable, the Creator will be notified of the reason. Delisting stops new sales of the Work; accrued Creator Share on completed sales remains payable.

4. Copyright & License Grant

4.1 Creators Retain Copyright

Creators retain full copyright in their Works. Nothing in these Terms assigns or transfers ownership of a Work to PUNICODEX, to a Buyer, or to any other party.

4.2 Merch Consent License

Merch Consent is strictly opt-in: the consent checkbox is unchecked by default for every Work, and no Work is printed on merchandise without it. By granting Merch Consent for a Work, the Creator grants PUNICODEX a non-exclusive, worldwide, sublicensable license to:

  • Reproduce the Work, including on made-to-order physical Products;
  • Display the Work across the Marketplace, temple pages, and Reliquary;
  • Sell the Work, including as print-on-demand merchandise through the Reliquary; and
  • Market the Work, including in storefront imagery, mockups, social posts, and promotional materials

in each case for as long as the Work remains listed with Merch Consent active. Sublicensing is limited to what is required to operate the fulfilment chain (for example, licensing the fulfilment partner to print the Work on ordered Products).

4.3 Withdrawal of Consent

Merch Consent may be withdrawn at any time from the creator dashboard. Withdrawal stops new sales of the affected Work within a commercially reasonable period (catalogue removal, mockup retirement, and fulfilment-cache clearance are not instantaneous). Withdrawal does not claw back completed sales: orders already placed, produced, or in production will be fulfilled, and the Creator Share on those sales remains payable.

4.4 No Other Rights Transfer

Except for the limited exhibition rights needed to display Works in the Marketplace and the license granted by an active Merch Consent, PUNICODEX claims no rights in any Work. Creators remain free to exhibit, license, and sell their Works elsewhere — the Merch Consent license is non-exclusive.

5. Revenue Share & Payouts

5.1 The 50/50 Split

For each sale of a Product bearing a Work with active Merch Consent, the Net Margin is split equally: 50% to the Creator (the Creator Share) and 50% to PUNICODEX.

5.2 How Net Margin Is Calculated

ComponentTreatment
Gross sale price received for the ProductIncluded
Shipping charges and taxes/duties collectedExcluded from the base
Fulfilment partner base production costDeducted
Payment processing fees on the transactionDeducted

Example: A hoodie sells for $38.00. The fulfilment partner's base cost is $21.50 and payment processing fees are $1.40. Net Margin = $38.00 − $21.50 − $1.40 = $15.10. The Creator Share is $7.55 and PUNICODEX retains $7.55.

5.3 University Credit Payouts (Phase 1)

In the current phase of the program, Creator Share payouts accrue to the Creator's institution account as university credit, administered through the Sponsored Institution. Direct payouts to Creators are planned for a later phase; these Terms will be updated, with notice, when direct payouts launch.

5.4 Earnings Transparency

Accrued and settled earnings are visible to the Creator at all times on the creator dashboard, including per-Work and per-sale breakdowns of Net Margin and Creator Share.

5.5 Taxes

Creators (or their institutions, as administrators of the credit) are responsible for any tax obligations arising from Creator Share earnings in their jurisdiction. PUNICODEX does not provide tax advice.
