The Pantheon

Pantheum

c. 126 CE · roman · STRATA 10 — The Adoption

Hadrian’s temple to all the gods, whose un-reinforced concrete dome remains the largest of antiquity and the oculus its open eye to heaven. Re-consecrated as a church, it is Rome’s best-preserved interior and a model of cosmic architecture.
