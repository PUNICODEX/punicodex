Open Roles

CAREERS

Help restore the names of the gods. PuniCodex is a small, standards-obsessed team — every hire matters, and every role touches the temples directly.

A note on tooling: fluency with AI tooling and agentic workflows is a strong plus in every role below. We build with agents daily — tell us how you use them, and where you still trust your own hand.

Social Media Marketer

Contract · Remote · Competitive — tell us your rate

Own the PuniCodex voice across X, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. Temple launches, new restorations, and a search engine for the Unicode web are the raw material — your job is to turn them into stories people actually share.

What you'd own

The content calendar end to end — concept, copy, posting cadence, community replies, and the monthly read on what worked. One voice, four platforms, zero filler.

What convinces us

  • Accounts you have grown yourself — with the numbers and the receipts.
  • A post or thread you are proud of, and a one-paragraph account of why it worked.
  • You can explain a macron to a general audience without dumbing it down.
  • You ship on a cadence without being chased.

Sponsorship Fulfilment (Business Outreach)

Contract · Remote · Competitive — tell us your rate

Own the pipeline from pattern-matched prospect lists to signed temple sponsorships. We know which brands rhyme with which gods; you turn that into conversations and contracts.

What you'd own

Outbound sequencing, follow-ups, CRM hygiene, and the forecast — first cold email to signed agreement. Commission-friendly structure for the right operator.

What convinces us

  • You can write a cold email a CEO actually answers — paste one into your application.
  • A pipeline you have run before: volumes, conversion, cycle length.
  • CRM discipline — your notes are good enough that someone else could close your deals.
  • You treat "no" as data, not drama.

Video Generation Expert

Contract · Remote · Competitive — tell us your rate

Produce cinematic short-form renderings of temples, mascots, and restorations — the pantheon in motion, cut for feeds and for the temples themselves.

What you'd own

Concept to final cut — promptcraft, shot design, sound design, and delivery in the formats each platform rewards. Runway/Pika/Sora-class tooling welcome; taste is the requirement.

What convinces us

  • A portfolio or reel — required, not optional.
  • You think in shots: you can storyboard a 20-second piece before you touch a tool.
  • Sound design is part of your work, not an afterthought.
  • You know the failure modes of AI video and how to cut around them.

University Partnership Liaison

Contract · Remote · Competitive — tell us your rate

Grow the Scholarly Edition — the university-edited layer of the flagship temples — one department at a time: professors, student reviewers, sponsored sections.

What you'd own

Outreach to classics, linguistics, and digital-humanities departments; the sponsorship application pipeline; and the relationships that turn one sponsored section into a standing collaboration.

What convinces us

  • You understand academic calendars, credibility, and how a "yes" actually happens in a classics department.
  • You have built partnerships in academia or adjacent institutions before — name them.
  • You write emails a professor finishes reading.
  • You respect peer review instead of routing around it.

The team is small enough that every application lands on one desk. Meet the founder before you write to it.
