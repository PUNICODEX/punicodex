### III. ARDIBEHI _S_ T[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_214) YA _S_ T.

This Ya _s_ t is for a great part devoted to the praise of the Airyaman prayer, which is described as driving away all the diseases and plagues that have been brought upon the world by Angra Mainyu; and when the writer passes from the glorification of Airyaman to that of Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, which is put into the mouth of Angra Mainyu himself (§§ 13 seq.), he makes him speak of Asha-Vahi _s_ ta just in the same way, and ascribe him just the same powers, as he himself has done with regard to Airyaman. This is owing to the fact of Airyaman being invoked in company with Asha-Vahi _s_ ta in the second formula of the Sîrôzah[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_215).

The powers ascribed to Asha-Vahi _s_ ta have their origin in the twofold nature of that Amesha-Spe _n_ ta, who being, in his abstract character, the impersonation of the highest element in Mazdeism, Divine Order and Holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_216), and in his concrete character, the genius who presides over the mightiest of physical elements, Fire[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_217), is one of the most powerful and dreaded opponents of Angra Mainyu[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_218). On the other hand, Airyaman is the genius to whom Ahura Mazda applied to heal the nine, and ninety, and nine hundred and nine thousand diseases created by Angra Mainyu[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_219).

This Ya _s_ t is recited every day at the Gâhs Hâvan, Rapithwin, and Aiwisrûthrem (Anquetil).

0. May Ahura Mazda be rejoiced! . . . .

Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good . . . .

I confess myself a worshipper of Mazda, a follower of Zarathu _s_ tra, one who hates the Daêvas and obeys the laws of Ahura; For sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification unto [Hâvani], the holy and master of holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_220) . . . .

[p. 42](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)

Unto Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, the fairest; unto the much-desired Airyaman, made by Mazda, and unto the good Saoka, with eyes of love, made by Mazda and holy[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_221);

Be propitiation, with sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification.

Yathâ ahû vairyô: The will of the Lord is the law of holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_222). . . .

#### I.

1. Ahura Mazda spake unto Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra, saying: 'That thou mayest increase Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, O Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra! with hymns of praise, with performance of the office, with invocations, holy words, sacrifice, blessings, and adoration—once to abide in the shining luminous space, in the beautiful abodes[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_223)—for the sacrifice and invocation of us, the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_224)' . . . .

2. Zarathu _s_ tra said: 'Say unto me the right words, such as they are, O Ahura Mazda! that I may increase Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, with hymns of praise,

[p. 43](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)

with performance of the office, with invocations, holy words, sacrifice, blessings, and adoration,—once to abide in the shining luminous space, in the beautiful abodes,—for the sacrifice and invocation of you, the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas.

3[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_225). '. . . . I proclaim Asha-Vahi _s_ ta: if I proclaim Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, then easy is the way to the abode of the other Amesha-Spe _n_ tas[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_226), which Ahura Mazda keeps with Good Thoughts, which Ahura Mazda keeps with Good Words, which Ahura Mazda keeps with Good Deeds[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_227);

4. '(Easy is the way to the Garô-nmâna of Ahura Mazda): the Garô-nmâna is for the holy souls, and no one of the wicked can enter the Garô-nmâna and its bright, wide, holy ways; (no one of them can go) to Ahura Mazda.

#### II.

5. 'The Airyaman prayer[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_228) smites down the strength of all the creatures of Angra Mainyu, of the Yâtus and Pairikas[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_229). It is the greatest of spells, the best of spells, the very best of all spells; the

[p. 44](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)

fairest of spells, the very fairest of all spells; the fearful one amongst spells, the most fearful of all spells; the firm one amongst spells, the firmest of all spells; the victorious one amongst spells, the most victorious of all spells; the healing one amongst spells, the best-healing of all spells.

6. 'One may heal with Holiness, one may heal with the Law, one may heal with the knife, one may heal with herbs, one may heal with the Holy Word: amongst all remedies this one is the healing one that heals with the Holy Word; this one it is that will best drive away sickness from the body of the faithful: for this one is the best-healing of all remedies[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_230).

7. 'Sickness fled away [before it], Death fled away; the Daêva fled away, the Daêva's counter-work[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_231) fled away; the unholy Ashemaogha[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_232) fled away, the oppressor of men fled away.

8. 'The brood of the Snake fled away; the brood of the Wolf fled away; the brood of the Two-legged[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_233) fled away. Pride fled away; Scorn fled away; Hot Fever fled away; Slander fled away; Discord fled away; the Evil Eye fled away.

9. 'The most lying words of falsehood fled away; the _G_ ahi[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_234), addicted to the Yâtu, fled away; the

[p. 45](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)

[paragraph continues]_G_ ahi, who makes one pine[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_235), fled away; the wind that blows from the North[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_236) fled away; the wind that blows from the North vanished away.

10. 'He it is who smites me that brood of the Snake, and who might smite those Daêvas by thousands of thousands, by ten thousands of ten thousands; he smites sickness, he smites death, he smites the Daêvas, he smites the Daêva's counter-work, he smites the unholy Ashemaogha, he smites the oppressor of men.

11. 'He smites the brood of the Snake; he smites the brood of the Wolf; he smites the brood of the Two-legged. He smites Pride; he smites Scorn; he smites Hot Fever; he smites Slander; he smites Discord; he smites the Evil Eye.

12. 'He smites the most lying words of falsehood; he smites the _G_ ahi, addicted to the Yâtu; he smites the _G_ ahi, who makes one pine. He smites the wind that blows from the North; the wind that blows from the North vanished away.

13. 'He it is who smites me that brood of the Two-legged, and who might smite those Daêvas, by thousands of thousands, by ten thousands of ten thousands. Angra Mainyu, who is all death, the worst-lying of all Daêvas, rushed from before him

'For his brightness and glory I will offer unto him a sacrifice worth being heard[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_239), namely, unto Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, the fairest, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta. 'Yê _n_ hê hâtãm: All those beings of whom Ahura Mazda knows the goodness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[9](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_245) . . . .

'Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2308.htm#fn_247) . . . .
