### XIII. FARVARDÎN YA _S_ T.

The Fravashi is the inner power in every being that maintains it and makes it grow and subsist. Originally the Fravashis were the same as the Pit _ri_ s of the Hindus or the Manes of the Latins, that is to say, the everlasting and deified souls of the dead (see §§ 49-52); but in course of time they gained a wider domain, and not only men, but gods and even physical objects, like the sky and the earth, &c. (§§ 85-86), had each a Fravashi (see Ormazd et Ahriman, §§ 111-113).

This Ya _s_ t is to be divided into two parts. The former part (§§ 1-84) is a glorification of the powers and attributes of the Fravashis in general; the latter part (§§ 85–158) is an enumeration of the Fravashis of the most celebrated heroes of Mazdeism, from the first man, Gaya Maretan, down to the last, Saoshya _n_ _t_.

This latter part is like a Homer's catalogue of Mazdeism. The greatest part of the historical legends of Iran lies here condensed into a register of proper names. This enumeration is divided into seven chapters:

The first (XXIV, §§ 85-95) contains the names of several gods, of the first man, Gaya Maretan, the first law-giver, Zarathu _s_ tra, and his first disciple, Maidhyô-m _a_ ungha;

The second part (XXV, §§ 96-110) contains the names of the disciples of Zarathu _s_ tra, most of them belonging to the epical cyclus of Vî _s_ tâspa (Gu _s_ tâsp);

The third part (XXVI, §§ 111-117) is of uncertain character, and no name contained in it is found in the epical legends;

The fourth part (XXVII, §§ 118-128) seems to be devoted to the heroes of the other Karshvares and to mythical beings, born or unborn (cf. §§ 121, 122, 127, 128);

The fifth part (XXVIII, § 129) is devoted to Saoshya _n_ _t_ alone; The sixth part (XXIX, §§ 130-138) is devoted to the heroes before the time of Zarathu _s_ tra;

The seventh part (XXX, §§ 139-142) is devoted to the holy women of Mazdeism from Hvôvi, Zarathu _s_ tra's wife, down to Srûta _t_-fedhri, Vanghu-fedhri, and Ereda _t_-fedhri, the future mothers of his three unborn sons.

The second, third, and fourth enumerations all end with the

[p. 180](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

name of Astva _t_-ereta (that is to say, Saoshya _n_ _t_), which shows that they do not refer to successive generations, but to three independent branches, which are each developed apart down to the time of the Saviour.

0. May Ahura Mazda be rejoiced! . . . .

Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good . . . .

I confess myself a worshipper of Mazda, a follower of Zarathu _s_ tra, one who hates the Daêvas and obeys the laws of Ahura;

For sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification unto [Hâvani], the holy and master of holiness.

Unto the awful, overpowering Fravashis of the faithful; unto the Fravashis of the men of the primitive law[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_810); unto the Fravashis of the next-of-kin,

Be propitiation, with sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification.

Yathâ ahû vairyô: The will of the Lord is the law of holiness. . . .

#### I.

1. Ahura Mazda spake unto Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra, saying: 'Do thou proclaim, O pure Zarathu _s_ tra! the vigour and strength, the glory, the help and the joy that are in the Fravashis of the faithful, the awful and overpowering Fravashis; do thou tell how they come to help me, how they bring assistance unto me, the awful Fravashis of the faithful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_811).

2. 'Through their brightness and glory, O Zarathu _s_ tra! I maintain that sky, there above, shining and seen afar, and encompassing this earth all around.

3. 'It looks like a palace, that stands built of a

[p. 181](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

heavenly substance[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_812), firmly established, with ends that lie afar, shining in its body of ruby over the three-thirds (of the earth)[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_813); it is like a garment inlaid with stars, made of a heavenly substance, that Mazda puts on, along with Mithra and Rashnu and Spe _n_ ta-Ârmaiti, and on no side can the eye perceive the end of it.

4. 'Through their brightness and glory, O Zarathu _s_ tra! I maintain Ardvi Sara Anâhita, the wide-expanding and health-giving, who hates the Daêvas and obeys the laws of Ahura, who is worthy of sacrifice in the material world, worthy of prayer in the material world; the life-increasing and holy, the flocks-increasing and holy, the fold-increasing and holy, the wealth-increasing and holy, the country-increasing and holy[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_814);

5[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_815). 'Who makes the seed of all males pure, who makes the womb of all females pure for bringing forth, who makes all females bring forth in safety, who puts milk in the breasts of all females in the right measure and the right quality;

6. 'The large river, known afar, that is as large as the whole of all the waters that run along the earth; that runs powerfully from the height Hukairya down to the sea Vouru-Kasha.

7. 'All the shores of the sea Vouru-Kasha are boiling over, all the middle of it is boiling over,

[p. 182](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

when she runs down there, when she streams down there, she, Ardvi Sûra Anâhita, who has a thousand cells and a thousand channels; the extent of each of those cells, of each of those channels, is as much as a man can ride in forty days, riding on a good horse.

8. 'From this river of mine alone flow all the waters that spread all over the seven Karshvares; this river of mine alone goes on bringing waters, both in summer and in winter. This river of mine purifies the seed in males, the womb in females, the milk in females’ breasts[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_816).

9. 'Through their brightness and glory, O Zarathu _s_ tra! I maintain the wide earth made by Ahura, the large and broad earth, that bears so much that is fine, that bears all the bodily world, the live and the dead, and the high mountains, rich in pastures and waters;

10. 'Upon which run the many streams and rivers; upon which the many kinds of plants grow up from the ground, to nourish animals and men, to nourish the Aryan nations, to nourish the five kinds of animals[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_817), and to help the faithful.

11. 'Through their brightness and glory, O Zarathu _s_ tra! I maintain in the womb the child that has been conceived, so that it does not die from the

[p. 183](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

assaults of Vîdôtu[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_818), and I develop in it[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_819) the bones, the hair, the . . . . [](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_820), the entrails, the feet, and the sexual organs.

12. 'Had not the awful Fravashis of the faithful given help unto me, those animals and men of mine, of which there are such excellent kinds, would not subsist; strength would belong to the Dru _g_, the dominion would belong to the Dru _g_, the material world would belong to the Dru _g_.

13. 'Between the earth and the sky the immaterial creatures would be harassed by the Dru _g_; between the earth and the sky the immaterial creatures would be smitten by the Dru _g_; and never afterwards would Angra-Mainyu give way to the blows of Spe _n_ ta-Mainyu.

14. 'Through their brightness and glory the waters run and flow forward from the never-failing springs; through their brightness and glory the plants grow up from the earth, by the never-failing springs; through their brightness and glory the winds blow, driving down the clouds towards the never-failing springs.

15. 'Through their brightness and glory the females conceive offspring; through their brightness and glory they bring forth in safety; it is through their brightness and glory when they become blessed with children.

16. 'Through their brightness and glory a man is born who is a chief in assemblies and meetings[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_821), who listens well[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_822) to the (holy) words, whom Wisdom

[p. 184](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

holds dear[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_823), and who returns a victor from discussions with Gaotema, the heretic[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_824).

'Through their brightness and glory the sun goes his way; through their brightness and glory the moon goes her way; through their brightness and glory the stars go their way.

17. 'In fearful battles they are the wisest for help, the Fravashis of the faithful.

'The most powerful amongst the Fravashis of the faithful, O Spitama! are those of the men of the primitive law[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_825) or those of the Saoshya _n_ _t_ s[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_826) not yet born, who are to restore the world. Of the Others, the Fravashis of the living faithful are more powerful, O Zarathu _s_ tra! than those of the dead, O Spitama!

18. 'And the man who in life shall treat the Fravashis of the faithful well, will become a ruler of the country with full power, and a chief most strong; so shall any man of you become, who shall treat Mithra well, the lord of wide pastures, and Ar _s_ tâ _t_, who makes the world grow, who makes the world increase.

19. 'Thus do I proclaim unto thee, O pure Spitama! the vigour and strength, the glory, the help, and the joy that are in the Fravashis of the faithful,

[p. 185](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

the awful and overpowering Fravashis; and how they come to help me, how they bring assistance unto me, the awful Fravashis of the faithful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_827).'

#### II.

20. Ahura Mazda spake unto Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra, saying: 'If in this material world, O Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra! thou happenest to come upon frightful roads, full of dangers and fears, O Zarathu _s_ tra! and thou fearest for thyself, then do thou recite these words, then proclaim these fiend-smiting words, O Zarathu _s_ tra!

21. '"I praise, I invoke, I meditate upon, and we sacrifice unto the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful. We worship the Fravashis of the masters of the houses, those of the lords of the boroughs, those of the lords of the towns, those of the lords of the countries, those of the Zarathu _s_ trôtemas[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_828); the Fravashis of those that are, the Fravashis of those that have been, the Fravashis of those that will be; all the Fravashis of all nations[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_829), and most friendly the Fravashis of the friendly nations;

22. '"Who maintain the sky, who maintain the waters, who maintain the earth, who maintain the cattle, who maintain in the womb the child that has been conceived, so that it does not die from the assaults of Vîdôtu, and develop in it the bones, the hair, the . . . ., the entrails, the feet, and the sexual organs[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_830);

23. '"Who are much-bringing, who move with

[p. 186](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

awfulness, well-moving, swiftly moving, quickly moving, who move when invoked; who are to be invoked in the conquest of good, who are to be invoked in fights against foes, who are to be invoked in battles;

24. '"Who give victory to their invoker, who give boons to their lover, who give health to the sick man, who give good Glory to the faithful man that brings libations and invokes them with a sacrifice and words of propitiation[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_831);

25. '"Who turn to that side where are faithful men, most devoted to holiness, and where is the greatest piety[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_832), where the faithful man is rejoiced[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_833), and where the faithful man is not ill-treated[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_834)."'

#### III.

26. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who are the mightiest of drivers, the lightest of those driving forwards, the slowest of the retiring[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_835), the safest[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_835) of all bridges, the least-erring[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_835) of all weapons and arms[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_836), and who never turn their backs[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_837).

27. At once, wherever they come, we worship them, the good ones, the excellent ones, the good, the strong, the beneficent Fravashis of the faithful. They are to be invoked when the bundles of baresma are tied; they are to be invoked in fights against foes, in battles[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_838), and there where gallant men strive to conquer foes.

[p. 187](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

28. Mazda invoked them for help, when he fixed the sky and the waters and the earth and the plants; when Spe _n_ ta-Mainyu fixed the sky, when he fixed the waters, when the earth, when the cattle, when the plants, when the child conceived in the womb, so that it should not die from the assaults of Vîdôtu, and developed in it the bones, the hair, the . . . ., the entrails, the feet, and the sexual organs[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_839).

29. Spe _n_ ta-Mainyu maintained the sky, and they sustained it from below, they, the strong Fravashis, who sit in silence, gazing with sharp looks; whose eyes and ears are powerful, who bring long joy, high and high-girded; well-moving and moving afar, loud-snorting[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_840), possessing riches and a high renown.

#### IV.

30. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful; whose friendship is good, and who know how to benefit; whose friendship lasts long; who like to stay in the abode where they are not harmed by its dwellers; who are good, beautiful afar[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_841), health-giving, of high renown, conquering in battle, and who never do harm first.

#### V.

31. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful; whose will is dreadful unto those who vex them; powerfully working and most beneficent; who in battle break the dread arms of their foes and haters.

[p. 188](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

#### VI.

32. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful; liberal, valiant, and full of strength, not to be seized by thought, welfare-giving, kind, and health-giving, following with Ashi's remedies, as far as the earth extends, as the rivers stretch, as the sun rises[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_842).

#### VII.

33. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who gallantly and bravely fight, causing havoc, wounding[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_843), breaking to pieces all the malice of the malicious, Daêvas and men, and smiting powerfully in battle, at their wish and will.

34. You kindly deliver the Victory made by Ahura, and the crushing Ascendant, most beneficently, to those countries where you, the good ones, unharmed and rejoiced, unoppressed and unoffended, have been held worthy of sacrifice and prayer, and proceed the way of your wish.

#### VIII.

35. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, of high renown, smiting in battle, most strong, shield-bearing and harmless to those who are true, whom both the pursuing and the fleeing invoke for help: the pursuer invokes

[p. 189](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

them for a swift race, and for a swift race does the fleer invoke them;

36. Who turn to that side where are faithful men, most devoted to holiness, and where is the greatest piety, where the faithful man is rejoiced, and where the faithful man is not ill-treated[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_844).

#### IX.

37. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who form many battalions, girded with weapons[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_845), lifting up spears, and full of sheen; who in fearful battles come rushing along where the gallant heroes[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_846) go and assail the Dânus[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_847).

38. There you destroy the victorious strength of the Turanian Dânus; there you destroy the malice of the Turanian Dânus; through you the chiefs[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_848) are of high intellect[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_849) and most successful; they, the gallant heroes the gallant Saoshya _n_ _t_ s[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_850), the gallant conquerors of the offspring of the Dânus chiefs of myriads, who wound with stones[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_851).

#### X.

39. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who rout the two wings of an army standing in battle array, who make the centre swerve, and swiftly pursue onwards, to help the faithful and to distress the doers of evil deeds.

#### XI.

40. We worship the good, strong, beneficent

[p. 190](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

[paragraph continues] Fravashis of the faithful; awful, overpowering, and victorious, smiting in battle, sorely wounding, blowing away (the foes), moving along to and fro, of good renown, fair of body, godly of soul, and holy; who give victory to their invoker, who give boons to their lover, who give health to the sick man[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_852);

41. Who give good glory to him who worships them with a sacrifice, as that man did worship them, the holy Zarathu _s_ tra, the chief of the material world, the head of the two-footed race, in whatever struggle he had to enter, in whatever distress he did fear;

42. Who, when well invoked, enjoy bliss in the heavens; who, when well invoked, come forward from the heavens, who are the heads[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_853) of that sky above, possessing the well-shapen Strength, the Victory made by Ahura, the crushing Ascendant, and Welfare[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_854), the wealth-bringing, boon-bringing, holy, well fed, worthy of sacrifice and prayer in the perfection of holiness.

43. They shed Satavaêsa[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_855) between the earth and the sky, him to whom the waters belong[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_856), who listens to appeals and makes the waters flow and the plants grow up, to nourish animals and men, to nourish the Aryan nations, to nourish the five kinds of animals[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_857), and to help the faithful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_858).

44. Satavaêsa comes down and flows between the earth and the sky, he to whom the waters belong, who listens to appeals and makes the waters and the plants grow up, fair, radiant, and full of

[p. 191](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

light, to nourish animals and men, to nourish the Aryan nations, to nourish the five kinds of animals, and to help the faithful.

#### XII.

45. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful; with helms of brass, with weapons of brass, with armour[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_859) of brass; who struggle in the fights for victory in garments of light, arraying the battles and bringing them forwards, to kill thousands of Daêvas.

When the wind blows from behind them[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_860) and brings their breath unto men,

46. Then men know where blows the breath of victory: and they pay pious homage unto the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, with their hearts prepared and their arms uplifted.

47. Whichever side they have been first worshipped in the fulness of faith of a devoted heart[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_861), to that side turn the awful Fravashis of the faithful, along with Mithra and Rashnu and the awful cursing thought[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_862) of the wise and the victorious wind.

48. And those nations are smitten at one stroke by their fifties and their hundreds, by their hundreds and their thousands, by their thousands and their tens of thousands, by their tens of thousands and their myriads of myriads, against which turn the awful Fravashis of the faithful, along with Mithra and Rashnu, and the awful cursing thought of the wise and the victorious wind.

[p. 192](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

#### XIII.

49. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who come and go through the borough at the time of the Hamaspathmaêdha[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_863); they go along there for ten nights, asking thus[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_864):

50. 'Who will praise us? Who will offer us a sacrifice? Who will meditate upon us? Who will bless us[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_865)? Who will receive us with meat and clothes in his hand[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_866) and with a prayer worthy of bliss[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_867)? Of which of us will the name be taken for invocation[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_868)? Of which of you will the soul be worshipped by you with a sacrifice[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_869)? To whom will this gift of ours be given, that he may have never-failing food for ever and ever?'

51. And the man who offers them up a sacrifice,

[p. 193](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

with meat and clothes in his hand, with a prayer worthy of bliss, the awful Fravashis of the faithful, satisfied, unharmed, and unoffended, bless thus:

52. 'May there be in this house flocks of animals and men! May there be a swift horse and a solid chariot! May there be a man who knows how to praise God[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_870) and rule in an assembly, who will offer us sacrifices with meat and clothes in his hand, and with a prayer worthy of bliss[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_871).'

#### XIV.

53. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who show beautiful paths to the waters, made by Mazda, which had stood before for a long time in the same place without flowing[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_872):

54. And now they flow along the path made by Mazda, along the way made by the gods, the watery way appointed to them, at the wish of Ahura Mazda, at the wish of the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas.

#### XV.

55. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who show a beautiful growth to the fertile[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_873) plants, which had stood before for a long time in the same place without growing:

56. And now they grow up along the path made

[p. 194](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

by Mazda, along the way made by the gods, in the time appointed to them, at the wish of Ahura Mazda, at the wish of the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas.

#### XVI.

57. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who showed their paths to the stars, the moon, the sun, and the endless lights, that had stood before for a long time in the same place, without moving forwards, through the oppression of the Daêvas and the assaults of the Daêvas[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_874).

58. And now they move around in their far-revolving circle for ever, till they come to the time of the good restoration of the world.

#### XVII.

59. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who watch over the bright sea Vouru-Kasha[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_875), to the number of ninety thousand, and nine thousand, and nine hundred, and ninety-nine.

#### XVIII.

60. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who watch over the stars Haptôiri _n_ ga[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_876), to the number of ninety thousand, and nine thousand, and nine hundred, and ninety-nine.

#### XIX.

61. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who watch over the body

[p. 195](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

of Keresâspa, the son of Sâma[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_877), the club-bearer with plaited hair, to the number of ninety thousand, and nine thousand, and nine hundred, and ninety-nine.

#### XX.

62. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who watch over the seed of the holy Zarathu _s_ tra[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_878), to the number of ninety thousand, and nine thousand, and nine hundred, and ninety-nine.

#### XXI.

63. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who fight at the right hand of the reigning lord, if he rejoices the faithful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_879) and if the awful Fravashis of the faithful are not hurt by him, if they are rejoiced by him, unharmed and unoffended.

#### XXII.

64. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who are greater, who are

[p. 196](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

stronger, who are swifter, who are more powerful, who are more victorious, who are more healing, who are more effective than can be expressed by words; who run by tens of thousands into the midst of the Myazdas.

65. And when the waters come up from the sea Vouru-Kasha, O Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra! along with the Glory made by Mazda[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_880), then forwards come the awful Fravashis of the faithful, many and many hundreds, many and many thousands, many and many tens of thousands,

66. Seeking water for their own kindred, for their own borough, for their own town, for their own country, and saying thus: 'May our own country have a good store and full joy!'

67. They fight in the battles that are fought in their own place and land, each according to the place and house where he dwelt (of yore)[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_881): they look like a gallant warrior who, girded up and watchful, fights for the hoard he has treasured up.

68. And those of them who win bring waters to their own kindred, to their own borough, to their own town, to their own country, saying thus: 'May my country grow and increase!'

69. And when the all-powerful sovereign of a country has been surprised by his foes and haters, he invokes them, the awful Fravashis of the faithful.

70. And they come to his help, if they have not been hurt by him, if they have been rejoiced by him, if they have not been harmed nor offended, the awful Fravashis of the faithful: they come flying unto him, it seems as if they were well-winged birds.

[p. 197](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

71. They come in as a weapon and as a shield, to keep him behind and to keep him in front, from the Dru _g_ unseen, from the female Varenya fiend, from the evil-doer bent on mischief, and from that fiend who is all death, Angra Mainyu. It will be as if there were a thousand men watching over one man[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_882);

72. So that neither the sword well-thrust, neither the club well-falling, nor the arrow well-shot, nor the spear well-darted, nor the stones flung from the arm shall destroy him.

73. They come on this side, they come on that side, never resting, the good, powerful, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, asking for help thus: 'Who will praise us? Who will offer us a sacrifice? Who will meditate upon us? Who will bless us? Who will receive us with meat and clothes in his hand and with a prayer worthy of bliss? Of which of us will the name be taken for invocation? Of which of you will the soul be worshipped by you with a sacrifice? To whom will that gift of ours be given, that he may have never-failing food for ever and ever[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_883)?'

74. We worship the perception[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_884); we worship the intellect; we worship the conscience; we worship those of the Saoshya _n_ _t_ s[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_885);

We worship the souls; those of the tame animals; those of the wild animals; those of the animals that live in the waters; those of the animals that live under the ground; those of the flying ones; those of the running ones; those of the grazing ones[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_886).

[p. 198](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

We worship their Fravashis[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_887).

75. We worship the Fravashis.

We worship them, the liberal;

We worship them, the valiant; we worship them, the most valiant;

We worship them, the beneficent; we worship them, the most beneficent;

We worship them, the powerful;

We worship them, the most strong;

We worship them, the light; we worship them, the most light;

We worship them, the effective; we worship them, the most effective.

76. They are the most effective amongst the creatures of the two Spirits, they the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful, who stood holding fast when the two Spirits created the world, the Good Spirit and the Evil One[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_888).

77. When Angra Mainyu broke into the creation of the good holiness, then came in across [Vohû](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/errata.htm#2) Manô and Âtar[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_889).

78. They destroyed the malice of the fiend Angra Mainyu, so that the waters did not stop flowing nor did the plants stop growing; but at once the most beneficent waters of the creator and

[p. 199](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

ruler, Ahura Mazda, flowed forward and his plants went on growing.

79. We worship all the waters;

We worship all the plants;

We worship all the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful.

We worship the waters by their names[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_890);

We worship the plants by their names[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_891);

We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful by their names.

80. Of all those ancient Fravashis, we worship the Fravashi of Ahura Mazda; who is the greatest, the best, the fairest, the most solid, the wisest, the finest of body and supreme in holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_892);

81. Whose soul is the Mãthra Spe _n_ ta, who is white, shining, seen afar; and we worship the beautiful forms, the active forms wherewith he clothes the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas; we worship the swift-horsed sun.

#### XXIII.

82. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis of the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas, the bright ones, whose looks perform what they wish, the tall, quickly coming to do, strong, and lordly, who are undecaying and holy;

83. Who are all seven of one thought, who are all seven of one speech, who are all seven of one deed; whose thought is the same, whose speech is the same, whose deed is the same, whose father and

[p. 200](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

commander is the same, namely, the Maker, Ahura Mazda;

84. Who see one another's soul thinking of good thoughts, thinking of good words, thinking of good deeds, thinking of Garô-nmâna, and whose ways[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_893) are shining as they go down towards the libations[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_894).

#### XXIV.

85. We worship the good, strong, beneficent Fravashis: that of the most rejoicing[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_895) fire, the beneficent and assembly-making[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_896); and that of the holy, strong Sraosha[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_897), who is the incarnate Word, a mighty-speared and lordly god; and that of Nairyô-sangha[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_898).

86. And that of Rashnu Razi _s_ ta[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_899);

That of Mithra[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_900), the lord of wide pastures;

That of the Mãthra-Spe _n_ ta[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[9](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_901);

That of the sky;

That of the waters;

That of the earth;

That of the plants;

That of the Bull[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[10](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_902);

That of the living man[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[11](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_903);

That of the holy creation[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[12](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_904).

87. We worship the Fravashi of Gaya Maretan[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[13](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_905),

[p. 201](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

who first listened unto the thought and teaching of Ahura Mazda; of whom Ahura formed the race of the Aryan nations, the seed of the Aryan nations.

We worship the piety and the Fravashi of the holy Zarathu _s_ tra;

88. Who first thought what is good, who first spoke what is good, who first did what is good; who was the first Priest, the first Warrior, the first Plougher of the ground[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_906); who first knew and first taught; who first possessed[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_907) and first took possession of the Bull[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_908), of Holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_909), of the Word, the obedience to the Word, and dominion, and all the good things made by Mazda, that are the offspring of the good Principle;

89. Who was the first Priest, the first Warrior, the first Plougher of the ground; who first took the turning of the wheel[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_910) from the hands of the Daêva and of the cold-hearted man; who first in the material world pronounced the praise of Asha[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_911), thus bringing the Daêvas to naught, and confessed himself a worshipper of Mazda, a follower of Zarathu _s_ tra, one who hates the Daêvas, and obeys the laws of Ahura.

90. Who first in the material world said the word that destroys the Daêvas, the law of Ahura; who first in the material world proclaimed the word that destroys the Daêvas, the law of Ahura; who

[p. 202](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

first in the material world declared all the creation of the Daêvas unworthy of sacrifice and prayer; who was strong, giving all the good things of life, the first bearer of the Law amongst the nations;

91. In whom was heard the whole Mãthra, the word of holiness; who was the lord and master of the world[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_912), the praiser of the most great, most good and most fair Asha[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_913); who had a revelation of the Law, that most excellent of all beings;

92. For whom the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas longed, in one accord with the sun, in the fulness of faith of a devoted heart; they longed for him, as the lord and master of the world, as the praiser of the most great, most good, and most fair Asha, as having a revelation of the Law, that most excellent of all beings;

93. In whose birth and growth the waters and the plants rejoiced; in whose birth and growth the waters and the plants grew; in whose birth and growth all the creatures of the good creations cried out, Hail[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_914)!

94. 'Hail to us! for he is born, the Âthravan, Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra. Zarathu _s_ tra will offer us sacrifices with libations and bundles of baresma; and there will the good Law of the worshippers of Mazda come and spread through all the seven Karshvares of the earth.

95. 'There will Mithra, the lord of wide pastures, increase all the excellences of our countries, and allay their troubles; there will the powerful Apãm-Napâ _t_[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_915) increase all the excellences of our countries, and allay their troubles.'

[p. 203](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

We worship the piety and Fravashi of Maidhyô-m _a_ ungha, the son of Arâsti[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_916), who first listened unto the word and teaching of Zarathu _s_ tra.

#### XXV.

96. We worship the Fravashi of the holy Asmô-_h_ _v_ anva _n_ _t_[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_917);

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Asan-_h_ _v_ anva _n_ _t_.

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Gavayan.

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Parsha _t_-g _a_ u _s_[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_918), the son of Frâta;

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Vohvasti, the son of Snaoya;

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Isva _t_, the son of Varâza.

97. We worship the Fravashi of the holy Saêna, the son of Ahûm-stu _t_[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_919), who first appeared upon this earth with a hundred pupils[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_920).

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Fradhidaya.

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Usmânara, the son of Paêshata.

[p. 204](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Vohu-rao _k_ ah, the son of Frânya;

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Ashô-rao _k_ ah, the son of Frânya;

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Varesmô-rao _k_ ah, the son of Frânya.

98. We worship the Fravashi of the holy Isa _t_-vâstra, the son of Zarathu _s_ tra;

We worship the Fravashi of the holy Urvata _t_-nara, the son of Zarathu _s_ tra;

We worship the Fravashi of the holy _H_ _v_ are-_k_ ithra, the son of Zarathu _s_ tra[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2318.htm#fn_921).
