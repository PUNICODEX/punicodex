### II. HAPTÂN YA _S_ T.

The Ya _s_ t of the seven Amshaspands is recited on the first seven days of the week, that is to say, on the days consecrated to the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas. In fact it is nothing more than an extract from the Sîrôzahs, being composed of the first seven formulas in their two forms: §§ 1-5 = Sîrôzah I, 1-7; §§ 6-10 = Sîrôzah II, 1-7. Then come four sections which are the original part of the Ya _s_ t (§§ 11-15).

______________________

0. May Ahura Mazda be rejoiced[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_187)! . . . .

Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_187) . . . .

I confess myself a worshipper of Mazda, a follower of Zarathu _s_ tra, one who hates the Daêvas and obeys the laws of Ahura;

For sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification unto [Hâvani], the holy and master of holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_187) . . . .

#### I.

1[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_188). To Ahura Mazda, bright and glorious, and to the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas;

To Vohu-Manô; to Peace, whose breath is friendly, and who is more powerful to destroy than all other creatures; to the heavenly Wisdom, made by Mazda, and to the Wisdom acquired through the ear, made by Mazda;

2[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_189). To Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, the fairest; to the much-desired Airyaman; to the instrument made by Mazda; and to the good Saoka, with eyes of love, made by Mazda and holy;

[p. 36](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)

To Khshathra-Vairya; to the metals; to Mercy and Charity.

3[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_190). To the good Spe _n_ ta-Ârmaiti, and to the good Râta, with eyes of love, made by Mazda and holy;

To Haurvatâ _t_, the master; to the prosperity of the seasons and to the years, the masters of holiness;

And to Ameretâ _t_, the master; to fatness and flocks; to the plenty of corn; and to the powerful Gaokerena, made by Mazda.

4[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_191). (At the Gâh Hâvan): To Mithra, the lord of wide pastures and to Râma _H_ _v_ âstra.

(At the Gâh Rapithwin): To Asha-Vahi _s_ ta and to Âtar, the son of Ahura Mazda.

(At the Gâh Uzîren): To Apãm Napâ _t_, the tall lord, and to water, made by Mazda.

5[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_192). (At the Gâh Aiwisrûthrem): To the Fravashis of the faithful and to the females that bring forth flocks of males; to the prosperity of the seasons; to the well-shapen and tall-formed Strength; to Verethraghna, made by Ahura, and to the crushing Ascendant.

(At the Gâh U _s_ ahin): To the holy, devout, fiend-smiting Sraosha, who makes the world grow; to Rashnu-Razi _s_ ta and to Ar _s_ tâ _t_, who makes the world grow, who makes the world increase[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_192);

Be propitiation, with sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification!

Yathâ ahû vairyô: The will of the Lord is the law of holiness . . . .

[p. 37](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)

### II.

6[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_193) We sacrifice unto Ahura Mazda, bright and glorious; we sacrifice unto the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas, the all-ruling, the all-beneficent.

We sacrifice unto Vohu-Manô, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta; we sacrifice unto Peace, whose breath is friendly, and who is more powerful to destroy than all other creatures; we sacrifice unto the heavenly Wisdom, made by Mazda; we sacrifice unto the wisdom acquired through the ear, made by Mazda.

7[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_194). We sacrifice unto Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, the fairest, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta; we sacrifice unto the much-desired Airyaman; we sacrifice unto the instrument made by Mazda; we sacrifice unto the good Saoka, with eyes of love, made by Mazda and holy.

We sacrifice unto Khshathra-Vairya; we sacrifice unto the metals; we sacrifice unto Mercy and Charity.

8[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_195). We sacrifice unto the good Spe _n_ ta-Ârmaiti; we sacrifice unto the good Râta, with eyes of love, made by Mazda and holy.

We sacrifice unto Haurvatâ _t_, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta; we sacrifice unto the prosperity of the seasons; we sacrifice unto the years, the holy and masters of holiness.

We sacrifice unto Ameretâ _t_, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta; we sacrifice unto fatness and flocks; we sacrifice unto the plenty of corn; we sacrifice unto the powerful Gaokerena, made by Mazda.

9[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_196). (At the Gâh Hâvan): We sacrifice unto

[p. 38](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)

[paragraph continues] Mithra, the lord of wide pastures; we sacrifice unto Râma _H_ _v_ âstra.

(At the Gâh Rapithwin): We sacrifice unto Asha-Vahi _s_ ta and Âtar, the son of Ahura Mazda.

(At the Gâh Uzîren): We sacrifice unto Apãm Napâ _t_, the swift-horsed, the tall and shining lord, the lord of the females; we sacrifice unto the holy waters, made by Mazda.

10[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_197). (At the Gâh Aiwisrûthrem): We sacrifice unto the good, powerful, beneficent Fravashis of the faithful; we sacrifice unto the females who bring forth flocks of males; we sacrifice unto the prosperity of the seasons; we sacrifice unto the well-shapen, tall-formed Strength; we sacrifice unto Verethraghna, made by Mazda; we sacrifice unto the crushing Ascendant.

(At the Gâh U _s_ ahin): We sacrifice unto the holy, tall-formed, fiend-smiting Sraosha, who makes the world grow, the holy and master of holiness; we sacrifice unto Rashnu-Razi _s_ ta; we sacrifice unto Ar _s_ tâ _t_, who makes the world grow, who makes the world increase.

#### III.

11. Let the Yâtus be crushed, O Zarathu _s_ tra[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_198)! both Daêvas and men[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_199).

Who is he in whose house, O Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra! every Dru _g_ is destroyed, every Dru _g_ perishes, when he pronounces these words[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_200):

12[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_201) . . . . . . . . . ?

13. It is he who takes the seven Amesha-Spe _n_ tas,

[p. 39](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)

the all-ruling, the all-beneficent, as a shield[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_202) against his enemies.

We worship the Law of the worshippers of Mazda; we worship the waters coming in the shape of a horse[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_203), made by Mazda.

14-15. He has renounced trespasses and faults, O Zarathu _s_ tra! he has renounced all trespasses and faults[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_204), O Zarathu _s_ tra! when he throws down[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_205) the destroyer of Vohu-Manô and his words[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_206), with a hundred times hundredfold, with a many times manifold preaching and smiting, and he takes away the Law of Mazda, that was carried away as a prisoner[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_207), from the hands of the [ungodly], who are destroyed by his strength.

Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good. . . .

16. Yathâ ahû vairyô: The will of the Lord is the law of holiness . . . .

I bless the sacrifice and the prayer, the strength and vigour

Of Ahura Mazda, bright and glorious, and of the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas;

Of Vohu-Manô; of Peace, whose breath is friendly[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_208) . . . .

[p. 40](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)

Of Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, the fairest; of the much-desired Airyaman[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_209) . . . .

Of Khshathra-Vairya, of the metals[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_209) . .

Of the good Spe _n_ ta-Ârmaiti and of the good Râta[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_210). . . .

Of Haurvatâ _t_, the master[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_210) . . . .

Of Ameretâ _t_, the master[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_210) . . . .

(At the Gâh Hâvan): Of Mithra[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_211) . . . .

(At the Gâh Rapithwin): Of Asha-Vahi _s_ ta[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_211) . . .

(At the Gâh Uzîren): Of the high lord Apãm Napâ _t_[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_211) . . . .

(At the Gâh Aiwisrûthrem): Of the Fravashis of the faithful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_212) . . .

(At the Gâh U _s_ ahin): Of the holy, devout, fiend-smiting Sraosha, who makes the world grow; of Rashnu-Razi _s_ ta and of Ar _s_ tâ _t_, who makes the world grow, who makes the world increase.

Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good . . .

Give unto that man[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2307.htm#fn_213) brightness and glory, give him health of body, give him sturdiness of body, give him victorious strength of body, give him full welfare of wealth, give him a virtuous offspring, give him long, long life, give him the bright, all-happy, blissful abode of the holy Ones.
