### IV. KHORDÂD YA _S_ T.

Only the first two sections of this Ya _s_ t refer to its nominal object, Haurvatâ _t_, the Genius of Health and Waters (Vend. Introd. IV, 7, 33). The rest of the Ya _s_ t refers to the performance of the Bareshnûm ceremony as being the test of the true Zoroastrian. As the Bareshnûm purification was performed by Airyaman to drive away the myriads of diseases created by Angra Mainyu[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_249), its laudation is not quite unaptly inserted in a Ya _s_ t devoted to the Genius of Health.

The Khordâd Ya _s_ t can be recited at any time. It is better to recite it during the Gâh U _s_ ahin, on the day Khordâd (Anquetil).

The text is corrupt.

_____________________

0. May Ahura Mazda be rejoiced! . . . .

Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good . . . .

I confess myself a worshipper of Mazda, a follower of Zarathu _s_ tra, one who hates the Daêvas and obeys the laws of Ahura;

For sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification unto [Hâvani], the holy and master of holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_250) . . . . .

Unto Haurvatâ _t_, the master; unto the prosperity

[p. 49](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)

of the seasons and unto the years, the masters of holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_251),

Be propitiation, with sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification.

Yathâ ahû vairyô: The will of the Lord is the law of holiness . . . .

We sacrifice unto Haurvatâ _t_, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta; we sacrifice unto the prosperity of the seasons; we sacrifice unto the years, the holy and masters of holiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_252).

1. Ahura Mazda spake unto Spitama Zarathu _s_ tra, saying: 'I created for the faithful the help, the enjoyments, the comforts, and the pleasures of Haurvatâ _t_. We unite them with him who would come up to thee as one of the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas, as he would come to any of the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas, Vohu-Manô, Asha-Vahi _s_ ta, Khshathra-Vairya, Spe _n_ ta-Ârmaiti, Haurvatâ _t_, and Ameretâ _t_.

2. 'He who against the thousands of thousands of those Daêvas, against their ten thousands of ten thousands, against their numberless myriads would invoke the name of Haurvatâ _t_, as one of the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas, he would smite the Nasu, he would smite Ha _s_ i[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_253), he would smite Ba _s_ i[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_253), he would smite Saêni[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_253), he would smite Bû _g_ i[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_253).

3[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_254). 'I proclaim the faithful man as the first [of men]; if I proclaim the faithful man as the first

[p. 50](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)

[paragraph continues] [of men][](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_255), then Rashnu Razi _s_ ta[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_256), then every heavenly Yazata of male nature in company with the Amesha-Spe _n_ tas will free the faithful man[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_257)

4. 'From the Nasu, from Ha _s_ i, from Ga _s_ i[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_258), from Saêni, from Bû _g_ i; from the hordes with the wide front, from the hordes with the many spears uplifted, from the evil man who oppresses, from the wilful sinner[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_259), from the oppressor of men, from the Yâtu, from the Pairika, from the straying way.

5. 'How does the way of the faithful turn and part from the way of the wicked[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_260)?'

Ahura Mazda answered: 'It is when a man pronouncing my spell, either reading[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_261) or reciting it by heart, draws the furrows[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_262) and hides[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[9](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_263) there himself, [saying]:

6. "I will smite thee, O Dru _g_! whomsoever thou art, whomsoever thou art amongst the Dru _g_ es that come in an open way, whomsoever thou art amongst the Dru _g_ es that come by hidden ways, whomsoever thou art amongst the Dru _g_ es that defile by contact; whatsoever Dru _g_ thou art, I smite thee away from the Aryan countries; whatsoever Dru _g_ thou art, I bind thee; I smite thee down, O Dru _g_! I throw thee down below, O Dru _g_!"

[p. 51](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)

7. 'He draws [then] three furrows[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_264): I proclaim him one of the faithful; he draws six furrows[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_264): I proclaim him one of the faithful; he draws nine furrows[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_264): I proclaim him one of the faithful.

8. 'The names of those (Amesha-Spe _n_ tas) smite the men turned to Nasus[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_265) by the Dru _g_ es; the seed and kin of the deaf[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_266) are smitten, the scornful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_267) are dead, as the Zaotar Zarathu _s_ tra blows them away to woe[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_268), however fierce, at his will and wish, as many as he wishes.

9. 'From the time when the sun is down he smites them with bruising blows; from the time when the sun is no longer up, he deals deadly blows on the Nasu with his club struck down, for the propitiation and glorification of the heavenly gods.

10. 'O Zarathu _s_ tra! let not that spell be shown to any one, except by the father to his son, or by the brother to his brother from the same womb, or by the Âthravan to his pupil[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_269) in black hair, devoted to the good law, who, devoted to the good law, holy[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_270) and brave, stills all the Dru _g_ es[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_271).

11. 'For his brightness and glory, I will offer unto him a sacrifice worth being heard, namely, unto Haurvatâ _t_, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta. Unto Haurvatâ _t_, the Amesha-Spe _n_ ta, we offer up the libations, the Haoma and meat, the baresma, the wisdom of the tongue, the

[p. 52](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)

holy spells, the speech, the deeds, the libations, and the rightly-spoken words.

12. 'Yathâ ahû vairyô: The will of the Lord is the law of holiness . . . .

'I bless the sacrifice and prayer, and the strength and vigour of Haurvatâ _t_, the master; of the prosperity of the seasons and of the years, the masters of holiness.

'Ashem Vohû: Holiness is the best of all good. . . . .

'[Give] unto that man[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe23/sbe2309.htm#fn_272)' brightness and glory, . . . . give him the bright, all happy, blissful abode of the holy Ones.'
