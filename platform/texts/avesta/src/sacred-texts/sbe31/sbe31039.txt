### YASNA XVII.

#### TO THE FIRES, WATERS, PLANTS, &c.

1-10[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1081), 11. We worship thee, the Fire, O Ahura Mazda's son! We worship the fire Berezi-savangha (of the lofty use[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1082)), and the fire Vohu-fryâna (the good and friendly[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1083)), and the fire Urvâzi _s_ ta (the most beneficial and most helpful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1084)), and the fire Vâzi _s_ ta (the most supporting[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1085)), and the fire Sp _e_ ni _s_ ta (the most bountiful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1086)), and Nairya-sangha the Yazad of the royal lineage[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1087), and that fire which is the house-lord of all houses and Mazda-made, even the son of Ahura Mazda, the holy lord of the ritual order, with all the fires.

12. And we worship the good and best waters Mazda-made, holy, all the waters Mazda-made and holy, and all the plants which Mazda made, and which are holy.

[p. 259](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)

13. And we worship the Mãthra-spe _n_ ta (the bounteous word-of-reason), the Zarathu _s_ trian law against the Daêvas, and its long descent.

14. And we worship Mount Ushi-darena which is Mazda-made and shining with its holiness, and all the mountains shining with holiness, and of abundant glory, and which Mazda made—.

15. And we worship the good and pious prayer for blessings, (16) and these waters and (these lands), (17) and all the greatest chieftains, lords of the ritual order[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1088);

18. And I praise, invoke, and glorify the good, heroic, bountiful Fravashis of the saints, those of the house, the Vîs, the Zantuma, the Da _h_ _v_ yuma[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1089), and the Zarathu _s_ trôtema, and all the holy Yazads[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31039.htm#fn_1090)!
