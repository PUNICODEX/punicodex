### YASNA XL.

#### PRAYERS FOR HELPERS.

1. And now in these Thy dispensations, O Ahura Mazda! do Thou wisely[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm#fn_1201) act for us, and with abundance

[p. 289](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)

with Thy bounty and Thy tenderness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm#fn_1202) as touching us; and grant that reward which Thou hast appointed to our souls, O Ahura Mazda! 2. Of this do Thou Thyself bestow upon us for this world and the spiritual; and now as part thereof (do Thou grant) that we may attain to fellowship with Thee, and Thy Righteousness for all duration.

3. And do Thou grant us, O Ahura! men who are righteous, and both lovers and producers of the Right as well. And give us trained beasts for the pastures, broken in for riding[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm#fn_1203), and for bearing, (that they may be) in helpful[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm#fn_1204) companionship with us, and as a source of long enduring vigour, and a means of rejoicing grace to us for this[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm#fn_1205).

4. So let there be a kinsman lord for us, with the labourers of the village, and so likewise let there be the clients (or the peers[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm#fn_1206)). And by the help of those may we arise.

So may we be to You, O Mazda Ahura! holy and true[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31055.htm#fn_1207), and with free giving of our gifts.
