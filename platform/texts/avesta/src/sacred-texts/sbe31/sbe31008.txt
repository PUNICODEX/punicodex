### YASNA XXX.

#### THE DOCTRINE OF DUALISM.

1. Accustomed to instruct the masses who throng him on public occasions seeking light, the composer constructs this hymn for similar opportunities. He may be regarded as continuing the thoughts in the close of Y. XXVIII, where he besought Ahura to inform him concerning the origin of the world. He says that he will declare the counsels of God, by which, as we see, he means the great doctrines concerning the origin of good and evil. With these he will declare also the praises, the laudatory portions of the Mãthra, and the sacrifices. And he prays that propitious results may be discerned in the heavenly bodies.

2. He further introduces what he has to say by telling the throngs before him that a decisive moment is upon them. They are to choose their religion, and not by acclamation with the foolish decision of a mob, but man by man, each individually for himself. They should therefore arouse themselves and hear with all attention, and gaze at the holy Fire with a good and receptive disposition of mind.

3. He then delivers the earliest statement of dualism which has come down to us. There were two original spirits, and they are called, be it well noted, not two persons, or at least not only two persons, but a better thing, or principle, and a worse one. (The qualifying words are all in the neuter[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_131).)

At the next sentence they are personified as a pair, each independent in his thoughts, declarations, and actions. Such is the short Theodicy, followed at once by an admonition to those before him to choose the better.

4. These two spirits came together as by natural combination, to make the opposing phenomena of life and its absence, of Heaven and of Hell.

And Hell is described not as a scene of cruelty inflicted on the innocent and the ignorant, but as 'the worst life,' and Heaven as equally remote from a superstitious paradise; that is, as the 'best mental state.'

[p. 26](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

This is the proper Zarathu _s_ trian creation. It is undeniably 'abstract,' very, and just in proportion as it lacks colour and myth are its depths visible. The account of it is also very limited. But it must never be forgotten that its existence is the probable proof that very much more of the kind existed beside it. Instead of there being one hymn sung like this, Y. XXX, there were probably many. The two original forces or beings, although separate clearly, come together; but they do not lose their distinction. Their difference remains as clear as their union. 5. They do not blend unrecognisably; for having created the two principles, they choose each his own particular realm. Ahura chooses the righteous order of religion, and with it the pious of all ages. The evil spirit chooses the wicked.

The point and meaning of the entire doctrine is that a good God cannot be responsible for permanent evil; that imperfection and suffering are original, and inherent in the nature of things, and permanently so. The swallowing up of sin and sorrow in ultimate happiness belongs to a later period. It is not Gâthic Zarathu _s_ trianism. Evil was the work of an independent being.

The great thinker saw his point; and it was that the Deity Himself could not prevent the evolution of base and revolting moral qualities with their consequent miseries in both victim and aggressor. An evil God was therefore their author.

6. But the blood-feuds of War, not to speak of the theological animosity, were too much for his philosophy. The sage could not regard all men and their circumstances with broad and equable impartiality.

The hated Daêva-worshippers, who were doubtless equally conscientious with the Zarathu _s_ trians, are said to have failed of correct discernment.

As they were deliberating, so he recalls, the Worst Mind, a very real although 'abstract' Satan, came upon them, to induce them to choose him and his evil realm. They acceded, becoming furious in their intention to injure human life. This may be regarded as a dramatic, but at the same time, in a moral sense, a philosophical statement of a temptation and fall. (For a later one, with more colour and less truth, see the temptation proper of Zarathu _s_ tra himself[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_132), recalling as it does so vividly the temptation in the Gospels.)

7. If we can accept the words ahmâi _k_ â to mean merely 'upon

[p. 27](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

this,' we may hold that the statements proceed without a break. Even a gap of lost verses does not interrupt the sense. The clothing of souls with bodies seems indicated. If so, the doctrine of the Fravashis, otherwise foreign to the Gâthas, may have its origin by inference here, and directly in verse 4. After the creation and first activity of the souls of the Archangels on the one hand, and of the Daêvas on the other, together with their respective human adherents, the one choosing good and the other evil, the remaining Ameshôspends unite with Âramaiti in bestowing a body upon the newly created soul. (So we must conclude from the language.) And the prophet breaks in with the prayer that in the future, and possibly at the Frashakar _d_, the completion of progress, these created souls might possess such advantages as they had when Ahura came at first with his acts of creation; that is, that they might be restored again to a state of sinless happiness, provided with bodies by Âramaiti as at the first. (See Ya _s_ t XIX, 89.)

8. But, as he implies, and perhaps expresses in a lost verse, vengeance shall come upon the wretched beings who choose the Evil Mind as their master. And it shall come, not in the abstract merely by any means, but as executed by a numerous, if not once predominant party, 'the offspring of the Evil Mind.' And when this shall have been completed (and XXXI, 18 shows us that the weapons to be used to bring it about were not to be those of verbal argument alone) then, as he declares with enthusiasm, 'to God shall be the Kingdom,' a Kingdom established in the Divine Benevolence, which will pervade its organic life, and which will likewise, as the personified 'Immortal,' utter encouragements and commands to its loyal citizens. And these citizens will then not only defeat the Lie-demon, who is the life of the Daêva-party, but they will deliver her up as a captive to the great Genius of Truth, the personified Righteousness. 9. And, as he ardently hoped for the coming of the Kingdom into the hands of Ahura, he as ardently beseeches that he and his coadjutors, the princes already named, may be honoured as the immediate agents in bringing on this 'millennial' completion; nay, he even prays that they may be as Ahuras[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_133) in merciful services, declaring that all their thoughts were centred in that scene where religious light dwelt as personified in her home.

10. Once more he announces the certain defeat and chastisement of the incarnate falsehood and her adherents, which enables

[p. 28](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

him only the more impressively to describe the rapid reunion of the righteous amid the home-happiness of Heaven.

11. Having delivered his brief but weighty communication, he commends his hearers for learning the holy vows of the Religion on account of the duration of the announced rewards and punishments. They shall be long indeed; and upon their complete inauguration full salvation shall be realised for those who shall have learned and heeded the invaluable truths.

Translation.

1. And now I will proclaim, O ye who are drawing near and seeking[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_134) to be taught! those animadversions[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_135) which appertain to Him who knows (all things) whatsoever; the praises which are for Ahura, and the sacrifices (which spring) from the Good Mind, and likewise the benignant meditations inspired by Righteousness. And I pray[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_136) that propitious results may be seen in the lights.

[p. 29](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

2. Hear ye then with your ears; see ye the bright flames[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_137) with the (eyes of the) Better Mind. It is for a decision as to religions, man and man, each individually for himself. Before the great effort of the cause, awake ye[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_138) (all) to our[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_139) teaching!

3. Thus are the primeval spirits who as a pair[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_140) (combining their opposite strivings), and (yet each) independent in his action, have been famed (of old). (They are) a better thing, they two, and a worse[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_141), as to thought, as to word, and as to deed. And between these two let the wisely acting choose aright. (Choose ye[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_142)) not (as) the evil-doers[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_143)!

[p. 30](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

4. (Yea) when the two spirits came together at the first to make[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_145) life, and life's absence[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_146), and to determine how the world at the last shall be (ordered), for the wicked (Hell) the worst life, for the holy (Heaven) the Best Mental State[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_147),

5. (Then when they had finished each his part in the deeds of creation, they chose distinctly each his separate realm.) He who was the evil of them both (chose the evil), thereby working[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_148) the worst of possible results, but the more bounteous spirit[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_149) chose the

[p. 31](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

[paragraph continues] (Divine) Righteousness; (yea, He so chose) who clothes upon Himself the firm[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_150) stones of heaven (as His robe). And He chose likewise them who content Ahura with actions, which (are performed) really in accordance with the faith[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_151).

6. And between these two spirits the Demon-gods (and they who give them worship) can make no righteous choice[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_152), since we have beguiled[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_153) them. As they were questioning and debating in their council[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_154) the (personified[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_155)) Worst Mind approached them that he might be chosen. (They made their

[p. 32](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

fatal decision.) And thereupon they rushed together unto the Demon of Fury, that they might pollute[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_156) the lives of mortals[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_157).

7. Upon this[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_158) Âramaiti (the personified Piety of the saints) approached, and with her came the Sovereign Power, the Good Mind, and the Righteous Order. And (to the spiritual creations of good and of evil) Âramaiti gave a body, she the abiding and ever strenuous[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_159). And for these (Thy people) so let[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_160) (that

[p. 33](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

body) be (at the last), O Mazda! as it was when Thou camest first with creations[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_161)!

8. And (when the great struggle shall have been fought out which began when the Daêvas first seized the Demon of Wrath as their ally[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_162)), and when the (just) vengeance shall have come upon these wretches, then, O Mazda! the Kingdom shall have been gained for Thee by (Thy) Good Mind (within Thy folk). For to those, O living Lord! does (that Good Mind[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_163)) utter his command, who will deliver the Demon of the Lie into the two hands[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_164) of the Righteous Order (as a captive to a destroyer).

9. And may we be such as those who bring on

[p. 34](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

this great renovation, and make this world progressive, (till its perfection shall have been reached). (As) the Ahuras of Mazda[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_165) (even) may we be; (yea, like Thyself), in helpful readiness to meet[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_166) (Thy people), presenting (benefits[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_167)) in union with the Righteous Order. For there[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_168) will our thoughts be (tending) where true wisdom shall abide in her home[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_169).

10. (And when perfection shall have been attained) then shall the blow of destruction fall upon the Demon of Falsehood, (and her adherents shall perish with her), but swiftest in the happy abode of the Good Mind and of Ahura the righteous saints

[p. 35](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)

shall gather, they who proceed in their walk (on earth) in good repute[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_170) (and honour)[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_171).

11. Wherefore, O ye men! ye are learning[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_172) (thus) these religious incitations which Ahura gave in (our) happiness[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_173) and (our) sorrow[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_174). (And ye are also learning) what is the long wounding for the wicked, and the blessings which are in store for the righteous. And when these (shall have begun their course), salvation shall be (your portion[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31008.htm#fn_175))!
