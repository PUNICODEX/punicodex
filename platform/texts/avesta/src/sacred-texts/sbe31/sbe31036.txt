### YASNA XIV (SP. XV).

#### DEDICATIONS.

1. I will come to You, O Ye Bountiful Immortals! as a praiser and a priest, and an invoker and sacrificer, as a memorising reciter and a chanter, for Your sacrifice and homage, which are to be offered to You, the Bountiful Immortals, and for our dedication and sanctification; (yea, for ours) who are the holy prophets (destined to benefit the saints).

2. And to You, O Ye Bountiful Immortals[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm)[2a](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm#fn_1062)! would I dedicate the flesh of my very body[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm#fn_1063), and all the blessings of a prospered life[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm#fn_1064).

3. In this Zaothra with this Baresman, I desire to

[p. 254](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm)

approach the holy Yazads with my praise[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm#fn_1065), and all the holy lords of the ritual order at their times, Hâvani at his time, and Sâvanghi and Vîsya at their times. 4. I confess myself a Mazdayasnian, and of Zarathu _s_ tra's order[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31036.htm#fn_1066).

5. The Zaotar speaks: As an Ahû (revered and) chosen, the Zaotar (?) speaks forth to me (?).

The Ratu speaks: As an Ahû (revered and) to be chosen, the Zaotar speaks forth to me.

The Zaotar: So let the Ratu from his Righteousness, holy and learned, speak forth!
