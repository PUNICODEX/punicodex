### YASNA XXXII.

#### THE STRUGGLE IS CONTINUED IN THE MIDST OF REVERSES.

1. The same author may well be supposed to continue. The first stanzas have been lost, but we observe that the subject of the section is still face to face with the Daêva party. He seems to see them arrayed and engaged in hostile devotions. But he is not intimidated. The friendship of Ahura is before his mind, and he expresses his desire that he and his colleagues may become, or continue, His apostles, notwithstanding the temporal sorrows which, according to XLIII, II, we see that he clearly anticipated as the portion of those who would propagate the holy faith.

2. Mazda answers him, and through him his followers, as established in His spiritual sovereignty, accepting the devotion of their piety with commendations and implied encouragements. He whom they would serve is supreme; they need not fear.

3. After reporting this response of Ahura, the composer turns with vehemence toward the Daêvas, poetically conceived to be present as if before their adherents, who also, according to verse 1, are supposed to be in sight (or are dramatically so conceived) celebrating their profane devotions; and he addresses them as the 'very seed' of Satan. Their worshippers belong to the religious falsehood and perversity. And they have persistently propagated their evil creed, which is in consequence spreading.

4. They have, so he acknowledges with grief, perverted men's minds, making them spokesmen for themselves, and in consequence deserters from the great Kindly Disposition of Ahura Mazda, and outcasts, fallen from His understanding.

5. They have destroyed the hopes of mankind for a happy life upon earth, and for Immortality in heaven. And in this they are not only the seed of the Evil Mind personified, but his servants rallying at his word.

6. Their leader is striving energetically, so he mournfully bewails, to effect his evil ends; but it is time that he should recall the counteracting measures of Ahura. His holy doctrines are to be announced, and their authority established by the divine Khshathra, His Sovereign Power personified.

[p. 55](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

7. The composer then contemplates with religious irony the infatuated security of the wretched delinquents whom he is apostrophising. Not a man of them knows the destruction which awaits him, and which, as he intimates, is close at hand, but Ahura, he significantly exclaims, is aware of it. And it will be proportionably severe. The blindness of sinners to danger seems as definite a judgment upon them in his estimate as their blindness to the truth.

8. To point his anger with an instance he names the apostate Yima, whom he supposes to have erred in first introducing the consumption of the flesh of cattle. He disavows community with him as with them all, declaring himself separate from them in Ahura's sight.

9. He acknowledges that their leader has to a certain degree defeated his teachings, and impaired the just estimates of life which he had striven to form within the people, (or that he will do this if not checked), declaring also that he had made inroads upon his property, which was sacred to the holy cause. And he cries aloud to Ahura and to Asha with the words of his very soul.

10. He repeats that their leader threatens to invalidate his teachings, blaspheming the supreme object of nature, the Sun, together with the sacred Kine, injuring the productive land, and carrying murder among the saints.

11. He utters his bitter wail in view of attempted slaughter, and actual spiritual opposition. He points out the plots among the powerful and their illegal confiscation of inheritances, as well those of women as those of men. And he declares that his opponents are endeavouring to injure his adherents, as if repelled by the best spiritual qualities which an individual could possess.

12. He announces the solemn judgment of God upon it all, especially reprobating those who deal treacherously against the mystical Kine; that is, the holy herds and people, and apostrophising those who prefer the Gr _e_ hma above the saving and sanctifying Asha, and the Kingdom of the Lie-demon above the Divine Khshathra.

13. He declares that Gr _e_ hma, an opposing chief, desired that evil kingdom in the abode of the personified Hell. And he cannot refrain from adding that he also enviously desires to share in the holy apostleship. But, as he severely rejoins, the messenger of God will hold him afar from the sight of the (Divine) Righteousness. He can have no share in the Faith.

(Here it may be noticed that we have some data for presenting

[p. 56](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

the main features of the struggle. In several instances, centring perhaps in the actual description of a battle in XLIV, 15, 16, we see traces of the closeness of the controversy. In XLIV, 15, the two hosts seem to be closing in regular lines for the 'holy vows themselves.' Here, on the other hand, we read of willing complaint or 'regretful desire,' while judicial blindness is referred to over and over again under various phrases. One might suppose that the Daêva-party were very near the Zarathu _s_ trians in many of their religious peculiarities, but that they could not accede to, or understand, the dualism. After the manner of Pagans they implicated the Gods in their sins. (Compare the drunken Indra.) At all events a bitter and violent war of doctrines was waging with both speech and weapons. I think it looks like the struggle 'of two parties' who each claimed to be the proper representative of some similar form of faith, similar, of course I mean, outwardly.)

14. Deploring the establishment of the Kavis who approach with stratagems and false teachings to aid the opposing party, the composer declares that they say that the Kine herself is to be injured instead of blessed by the very fire-priest who kindles[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_276) the altar-flame.

15. He supports himself however with the hope of ultimate success, and with the prospect of his reward, when he and his fellow-labourers should be gloriously borne to heaven by Weal and Immortality, the 'eternal two,' who not only, as we see, bear saints to bliss, but also constitute the beatitude of heaven itself.

16. He confides all at last to Ahura, who is able to control all events, and to solve all doubts, and who will support his servants in bringing the wicked to vengeance by means of verbal instructions and commands.

Translation.

(That rival-monarch (thus we may supply the sense of lost verses) for whom some are plotting to secure the sovereignty, and who, once in power, would deliver over home, village, town, and province to ruin and to death[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_277), is active in his efforts, and offering

[p. 57](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

the devotions of his false religion to accomplish his ends.) 1. His[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_278) lord-kinsman will pray[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_279) (as I Zarathu _s_ tra prayed), and his labouring villagers, with his (trusted) peers, and his (fellow) Daêva-worshippers[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_280). But in my mind is the friendship[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_281) of Ahura Mazda, the Great Creator, the living Lord; and Thine heralds, O Ahura! may we be; may we hold back[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_282) those who hate and who offend You!

2. To these (for whom the prophet spake) Ahura Mazda answered, ruling[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_283) as He does through His Good Mind (within their souls), He replied from His Sovereign Power, our good friend (as he is) through His surpassing[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_284) Righteousness: We have accepted

[p. 58](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

your good and bountiful Piety, and we have chosen her; ours shall she be[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_285)!

3. But you, O ye Daêvas! are all a seed from the Evil Mind[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_286). He who offers sacrifice[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_287) to You the most[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_288) is of the Lie-demon, and (he is a child) of perversion[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_289). In advance[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_290) (are your) deceits whereby ye are famed in the sevenfold[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_291) earth!

4. For ye (are) confusing our thoughts[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_292), whereby men, giving forth the worst deeds, will speak[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[9](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_293), as of

[p. 59](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

the Demon-gods beloved, forsaken by the Good Mind[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_294), (far) astray from the understanding of the Great Creator, the Living Lord, and (far astray) from His Righteousness!

5. Therefore ye would[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_295) beguile mankind of happy life[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_296) (upon earth) and of Immortality (beyond it), since the Evil Spirit (has ruled) you with his evil mind. Yea, he has ruled[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_297) you, (ye) who are of the Demon-gods, and with an evil word unto action, as his ruler[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_298) (governs) the wicked[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_299)!

[p. 60](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

6. Full of crime (your leader) has desired to destroy[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_300) us, wherefore he is famed, (and his doctrine is declared); but if this be so of these, then in the same manner, O Ahura! Thou possessest[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_301) (because Thou knowest) the true (teachings) in Thy memory[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_302). And in Thy kingdom and Thy Righteous Order I will establish Thy precepts (in Thy name)[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_303).

7. Among these wretched beings[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_304) (this their leader[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_305)) knows not that those things[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_306) which are declared

[p. 61](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

as victorious[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_307) (by his allies) are bound together for the smiting; yea, those things by which he was famed (as victorious) by his (blade of) glittering iron[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_308). But the utter destruction[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_309) of those things Thou, O Ahura Mazda! knowest[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_310), most surely[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_311)!

8. Of these wretched beings[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_312) Yima Vîvanghusha was famed to be; he who, desiring to content[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_313) our men, was eating kine's flesh in its pieces. But from[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_314) (such as) these, O Ahura Mazda! in Thy discerning discrimination, am I (to be seen as distinct[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[9](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_315)).

[p. 62](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

9. An evil teacher (as that leader is), he will[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_316) destroy (our) doctrines, and by his teachings he will pervert the (true) understanding of life, seizing away[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_317) (from me) my riches[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_318), the choice and real wealth of (Thy) Good Mind. To You and to Asha, O Ahura Mazda! am I therefore crying with the voice of my spirit's[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_319) (need)!

10. Aye, this man will destroy my doctrines (indeed, for he blasphemes the highest of creatures that live or are made). He declares that the (sacred) Kine[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_320) and the Sun are the worst of things which eye can see; and he will offer the gifts of the wicked (as priest to their Demon-gods). And at the last he will parch[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_321) our meadows with drought, and will hurl his mace at Thy saint (who may fall before his arms[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_322)).

[p. 63](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

11. Yea, these will destroy my life, for they consult with the great[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_323) of the wicked (enlightening themselves by their words[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_324)). And they are seizing away[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_325) the gifts of inherited treasures[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_326) from both household-lord and from house-wife[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_327); (wretched men that they are), and those who will fiercely wound (my folk, repelled and in no way kindly moved) by the better mind of the holy[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_328).

12. (But Ahura will speak His rebuke, for) as to those doctrines which (such) men may be (basely) delivering[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_329) (repelled) by the holiest action, (and galled[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_330) by its sacred truth) God hath said: Evil (are they! Yea, unto these He hath said it) who have slain the Kine's life by a blessing (and have cursed her while they offered to help her[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[9](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_331)), men by whom Gr _e_ hmas are loved above Righteousness, and the Karpans,

[p. 64](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

and the Throne of those who have wished[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_332) for the Demon of lies (as their deity and friend[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_333)).

13. And the Gr _e_ hma will seek[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_334) for these things by means of his (evil) kingdom[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_335) in the abode of (Hell which is[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_336)) the Worst Mind (who both are together) the destroyers of life, and who, O Mazda! will bewail[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_337) with glad but (envious) wish the message of Thy prophet. (But he will not abate with his vengeance), he will hold them afar from the sight[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[7](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_338) of the truth!

14. His is Gr _e_ hma[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[8](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_339); aye, his! And to (oppose) Thee[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[9](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_340) he will establish the Kavis and (their) scheming

[p. 65](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

plans. Their deeds[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_341) of power are but deceits since they have come as an aid to the wicked[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_342), and since he has been (falsely) said (to be set) to conquer the Kine[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_343), he who shall kindle that (very) help of grace which removes our death afar, (and lightens Thy saving flame).

15. And therefore will I drive from hence[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_344) the Karpans’ and Kavis’ disciples. And after these (have thus been driven hence and away) then these (my princely aiding saints) whom they (now) render no longer rulers at will over life, (and deprive of their absolute power), these shall be borne (at

[p. 66](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

last) by the (immortal[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_345)) two to the home of (Thy) Good Mind (in Heaven)[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_346)!

16. (And) this entire[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_347) (reward of the righteous) is from that Best One who teaches[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_348) in the wide (mental) light of the pious[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_349), ruling (as supreme), O Mazda Ahura[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_350)! whose are my woes and my

[p. 67](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)

doubtings[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_351) (yea, they lie in His power to heal), when I shall make (my prophets) men to be sought[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fn_352) for the harm of the wicked. And this I shall do by the word of my mouth (to defend and avenge my saints)!

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[65:1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_342) The predecessors of the Pahlavi translator seem to have understood the word var(e)_k_ _a_ u as conveying the idea of power rather than that of brilliancy. He renders freely pavan zak î varzânân avârûnŏ dânâkânŏ. Supposing the text to stand, and not supplying a formation from var(e)z, we may hold that there existed a var(e)_k_ beside var(e)z, as there undoubtedly was a har(e)_k_ (see harek _e_) beside har(e)z. This casts light on the Vedic vár _k_ as.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[65:2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_343) Amati _k_ pa _d_ îrênd valman darvandân aîyyârîh [ ] amati _k_ avŏ Tôra zani _s_ nŏ gûftŏ. The sufferings of the sacred Kine form the central thought of much that occurs.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[65:3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_344) Can gâu _s_ be a genitive here? But if a nominative, must not y _e_ refer to it? How then could the Kine 'kindle' the aid of grace? A genitive looks difficult. It is, however, accepted by Spiegel, although he renders differently from my translation. The Pahlavi may give us invaluable relief here by restoring the text. The ancient translator read vao _k_ aya _t_. Reading with him, we might render: When the Kine which (yâ?) caused a death-removing help to be declared, was said to be meet for subjection (or slaying, reading an infinitive from _g_ an). This rendering is more probable than that from sao _k_ aya _t_. The Kine distinctly caused this help to be declared. See XXIX. But I make it a matter of principle to follow the MSS. in a first translation, where that is at all possible.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[65:4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_345) The Pahlavi translator differs greatly here, having taken anâi _s_ with adverbial force, and as possessing the a priv. (they being [p. 66](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm) not inclined). He also read somewhat as follows: anâi _s_ avaênî(?) as y _e_ = from his non-inclination he was blind who (belongs to the Karpan and to the Kavi). Whether a truer text is indicated by him here is doubtful on account of XLIV, 13, and its nâshâmâ; but the unvarying explanation of the Kavis as blind probably derives its origin from some such reading here, or elsewhere in lost documents.

Certainly if âi _s_ can be used as a particle, anâi _s_ is not altogether impossible in some such sense. Moreover, the Pahlavi translation here and elsewhere has afforded us such a multitude of valuable concretes, that we shall do well to think twice before we reject its most startling suggestions. Lit. trl. 'what (things are) of the K.'

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[66:1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_346) The Pahlavi translation gives a fine suggestion in the concrete sense here; seeing the dual âbyâ, it explains it as referring to Haurvatâ _t_ and Ameretatâ _t_, which is very probably correct. So Spiegel also renders. It is very difficult to decide in which sense y _e_ _n_ g dai _n_ tî nôi _t_ jyât _e_ u _s_ khshayaman _e_ _n_ g vasô is to be taken. If in an evil sense (as vas _e_-khshaya _n_ t is sometimes elsewhere taken) one might think of such a rendering as this: I have driven the Karpans' and Kavis' disciples hence to those (evil rulers) whom they (my servants) render no longer wanton tyrants over life. But these (my champion saints) shall be borne by the two to the home of Thy Good Mind. But strict grammar demands of us that tôi should refer back to y _e_ _n_ g. Accordingly I suggest as above first.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[66:2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_347) Observe that Vohu Manah equals heaven. Recall XXX, 4, 'but for the holy Vahi _s_ ta Manah; that is heaven.'

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[66:3](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_348) The Pahlavi has ham; Neryosangh has sarvam.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[66:4](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_349) Reading sâ _k_(a)yãs _k_ î _t_ (P 11, s _k_ yas _k_ î _t_; Pahlavi, âmûkhti _s_ nŏ (sic); Ner., _s_ ikshâpa _n_ a _m_). Otherwise syas _k_ î _t_, which may well mean 'lying, reposing' in the wide (mental) light of the pious (or of the offering). Geldner lately admirably suggests a 2nd sg.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[66:5](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_350) Pavan farâkhû hûshîh.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[66:6](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_351) If this 'best one' is the Ratu of XXXIII, 1, all is grammatically clear; but the expressions are rather strong in view of [p. 67](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm) XLVIII, 9, where similar language is certainly applied to Ahura. If Ahura is here meant, we have only one instance more to add to the many in which Ahura is spoken of in the third person, with an address to Him thrown in. See the differing views of XLV, 11. Possibly the 'Best One' was Ahura's Spe _n_ ta Mainyu.

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[67:1](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_352) Zak î pavan gûmânîkîh. As to âithi, âithiva _n_ t seems to prove that its meaning must be calamity also in this place. Otherwise one is strongly tempted to heed the vigorous indication of the Pahlavi translator. Here and in XLVIII, 9, he renders 'manifest,' 'what is clear in the midst of my doubt.' The etymology would be far simpler. Alternatively dvaêthâ = terror (bî).

[](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm)[67:2](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/sbe31/sbe31010.htm#fr_353) Valman î pûmman khvâstâr. The Pahlavi sees 'to be desired' in ishy _e_ _n_ g. Otherwise one might render: I will cause (verbal) missiles (comp. zastâ-i _s_ tâi _s_) to be cast forth from the mouth for the harm of the wicked.

(SUPPLEMENTARY NOTE. 'Parch with drought' in verse 10 may be regarded as having figurative application. The destruction of the means of irrigation, so often resorted to in the same regions later, would point also to a literal sense, but 'waste our meadows like drought' is a safer expression. See further vivâpa _t_, and vî âpem = vîyâpa _t_, viyâpem.)
