Cultural Significance

From ancient cult to modern Unicode

Ancient Domain

Avalokiteśvara belongs to the Buddhist tradition, where it is associated with compassion, mercy, bodhisattva.

Avalokiteśvara in Later Traditions

Avalokiteśvara appears within the Buddhist tradition and has influenced later retellings, artistic depictions, and scholarly study. The Unicode restoration preserves the name's distinctive orthography for contemporary use.

Modern Legacy

Avalokiteśvara continues to appear in scholarship, art, and popular reception as a significant figure of the Buddhist tradition. Restoring the name in Unicode returns the original marks that ASCII removes.

Unicode Restoration as Cultural Act

Restoring Avalokiteśvara in a domain name is more than orthographic accuracy. It is a statement that the internet should recognize the full range of human writing — not only the ASCII keyboard.

01How do you pronounce Avalokiteśvara?

In reconstructed pronunciation, Avalokiteśvara is /avalokiteshvara/ — approximately 'AVALOKITESHVARA' — pronounce the restored form with attention to the preserved diacritics and length..

02What does Avalokiteśvara mean?

Avalokiteśvara means Lord who looks down in compassion in the buddhist tradition.

03What are the symbols of Avalokiteśvara?

Avalokiteśvara is associated with Restored Name (The Unicode form Avalokiteśvara carries the original marks of the Buddhist tradition.), Source Script (Attested in the original script as अवलोकितेश्वर.), Domain (Compassion, Mercy, Bodhisattva), Etymology (Lord who looks down in compassion).

04Why restore Avalokiteśvara in Unicode?

Plain ASCII avalokiteshvara strips the stress, length, and script that make the name specific. Unicode restoration returns the name to its original written dignity.

Avalokiteśvara is attested in the canonical Buddhist sources as a figure associated with compassion, mercy, bodhisattva.
