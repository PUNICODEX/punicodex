Ahriman ​(ahriman) ‌— Evil, ‌Destruction, ​Lies ​· Evil ​spirit, ​destructive ​mind — ‌belongs ​to ​the ‌Zoroastrian tradition, ​where ‌it ​is catalogued ‌under ​the ​domain ​"Evil, Destruction, ​Lies". ​The ‌name means ​"Evil ‌spirit, ​destructive mind".

‌Ahriman ​is ‌evil, ​destruction, lies. ‌The ​name ​means "Evil ‌spirit, ‌destructive ‌mind" ​and belongs ​to ‌the ​zoroastrian tradition.

Original Script & Provenance

Contributed by PuniCodex Team

Original writing system, transliteration steps, uncertainty markers, font/display notes.

The ​name ‌is ‌preserved ​in ​Avestan ​as 𐬀𐬢𐬭𐬀 ​𐬨𐬀𐬌𐬥𐬌𐬌𐬎 ​— ‌Avestan ​alphabet ​(Iranian), ‌attested ​Avestan ‌canon, ​c. ‌1000 ​BCE ​– ​600 CE ​(written ​tradition ‌4th–6th ​c. ‌CE), ​in ‌Eastern ​Iran. ‌The ​script ‌is ​written right-to-left.[1]

​The ‌scholarly ‌transliteration ‌is ​aŋra ​mainiiu ‌(Avestan ​scholarly), ​giving ‌the ​normalized ​reading ‌/aŋˈra ˈmaɪ.niː.ju/.

​The ‌rendering ​proceeds ​step ​by ​step:

‌- ‌Written ​aŋra ‌mainiiu ​in ​the ‌Avesta (Gathic ‌nom. ‌aŋrō ‌mainiiuš, ​Yasna ‌45.2; ​Younger ​form ​at ‌Vendidad ​19.1)
​- ​The Younger ‌spelling ​writes ​the ​ending ‌with ​two ‌i-letters ​(…mainiiu)
‌- ​'Ahriman' ‌is ‌the ​Middle Persian ​(Pahlavi) ​development ​of ‌the ‌same ​name
