The Test

Seat 1: Insurance & Protection Services

Claim: Protector of children and travelers; the guardian at every roadside. Exhibit: The Original Vow — The Kṣitigarbha Sutra recounts that in a previous life the Bodhisattva was a Brahmin maiden whose mother had slandered the Dharma.

Verdict: survives scrutiny. The myth and the trade do the same work.

Seat 2: Wedding, Family & Maternity

Claim: Protector of children; the beloved guardian of the young. Exhibit: Sai no Kawara — On the banks of Sai no Kawara, the riverbed between life and rebirth, the souls of dead children are forced to pile stones as penance.

Verdict: holds under load. The correspondence is functional, not decorative.

Seat 3: Faith & Spiritual Organizations

Claim: Roadside devotion across Japan; offerings still pile at his statues. Exhibit: The Monk Who Was Jizō — Countless Japanese tales tell of a wandering monk who helps a traveller in distress, only to disappear, leaving behind a statue of Jizō.

Verdict: survives scrutiny. The myth and the trade do the same work.

Verdict: survives scrutiny. The myth and the trade do the same work.
