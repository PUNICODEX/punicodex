The Authentic Name

Unicode restoration and ASCII comparison

Original Script

豊玉毘売命

The name in its original Japanese form. Toyotama (豊玉毘売命) is attested in the source tradition — “Abundant jewel”. Its original diacritics and script distinctions carry the full phonetic and orthographic weight of the source tradition.

ASCII Constraint

toyotama

The plain toyotama form is identical to the Unicode restoration. Because this name is already written in Latin letters, no diacritics, stress, or script information were lost — only capitalization differs.

Unicode Restoration

Toyotama

The Unicode restoration does not need to recover lost marks for Toyotama. Its value is canonical spelling and consistent cataloguing, not the reconstruction of erased orthography. The domain is readable as-is to both DNS and humanity.

Punycode Encoding
Toyotama.com → toyotama.com

The non-ASCII characters in Toyotama are encoded while the ASCII remains visible. To the DNS, it is Punycode. To humanity, it is Toyotama.

How Toyotama was spoken

/to.jo.ta.ma/ Modern Standard Japanese (Hepburn)
To- Voiceless alveolar stop [t] plus open-mid [o]
-yo- Palatal approximant [j] plus open-mid [o]; from 豊 'abundant'
-ta- Voiceless alveolar stop [t] plus open central [a]; from 玉 'jewel'
-ma Bilabial [m] plus open central [a]

Modern Approximation

'TOH-yoh-tah-mah' — four even syllables, with the second beginning with a soft 'y'.

05

Abundant Jewel Princess

Sea, Transformation, and the Mother of Emperors

Toyotama is the daughter of the sea god Ryūjin and the wife of Hoori. Her name, 'Abundant Jewel,' evokes the treasures of the undersea palace. She is the mother of Ugayafukiaezu, making her an ancestor of Emperor Jimmu and a bridge between the ocean depths and the imperial line.

Sea Princess

Daughter of Ryūjin and inhabitant of the undersea palace.

Wife of Hoori

She married the descendant of Ninigi and bore his son.

Mother of Ugayafukiaezu

Her son continued the line to Emperor Jimmu.

Shape-Shifter

She could transform between human and sea-dragon form.

Sacred Symbols

Jewel The abundance and treasure of the sea
Sea palace (Ryūgū) The undersea realm where she was born
Dragon or crocodile Her true form, seen by Hoori when he broke his promise
Cormorant feather robe The garment that allowed travel between sea and land
06

Mythology

Stories of Toyotama

Toyotama's mythology is the story of a sea princess who joins the terrestrial line but cannot stay.

Kojiki

Marriage in the Sea Palace

When Hoori descended to the undersea palace seeking the lost fishhook, Ryūjin welcomed him and gave him Toyotama in marriage. For three years they lived in the palace, and Toyotama became pregnant. Hoori longed for the land, and she agreed to accompany him.

Kojiki

The Broken Taboo

Toyotama asked Hoori not to watch her give birth. She entered a sealed hut, but curiosity overcame him. He peeked inside and saw her in her true form — a sea dragon or crocodile — giving birth. Ashamed, she returned to the sea, leaving their son Ugayafukiaezu on land. She sent her sister Tamayori to nurse him.

Kojiki

Ancestor of Jimmu

Ugayafukiaezu grew up and married his aunt Tamayori. Their son was Kamuyamato-Iwarebiko, later Emperor Jimmu. Thus the sea princess Toyotama became the mother of the imperial line's immediate ancestor.

Extended Lore

Toyotama is the bride who must be trusted unseen. Her return to the sea teaches that love across boundaries requires restraint. The imperial line is born from a broken promise — a reminder that even sacred genealogies carry loss.
