The Collection

Óðinn through the eyes of sculptors, painters, and craftsmen across the ages

Odin — H. E. Freund, 1825–27. The All-Father stands in solemn power, spear Gungnir in hand, the source of Norse kingship.

Odin of Fogelberg — Bengt Erland Fogelberg, 1830. Carrara marble capturing the lord of the gods with Huginn and Muninn on his helmet.

Odin upon Yggdrasil — The god hangs from the World Tree for nine nights, spear-wounded, to seize the secret of the runes.

Odin — Lorenz Frølich, 1844. The Danish master's vision of the one-eyed god in watercolor and ink.

Wodan Heals Baldr's Horse — Emil Doepler, c. 1905. The All-Father as shaman and healer, wielding ancient Germanic magic.

Valkyrie — Peter Nicolai Arbo, 1864. One of Odin's choosers of the slain, riding through battlefields with spear and shield.
