Before the Office, the Story

The Great Plan. The Hongfan chapter of the Shangshu presents the five phases as the first of the 'Nine Categories' revealed to Yu the Great. Water, fire, wood, metal, and earth each have their nature: water soaks and descends, fire blazes and ascends, wood bends and straightens, metal yields and changes, earth sows and gathers. This is the earliest systematic statement of Wǔxíng cosmology.

The Yin-Yang and Five Phases School. The philosopher Zou Yan (c. 324–250 BCE) applied the five phases to history, arguing that dynasties rise and fall according to which phase they embody. Each new dynasty was thought to possess the virtue of the next phase, giving cosmic legitimacy to political revolution.
