ASCII Constraint

Reduced to plain asa, the name loses everything that made it specific: original diacritics and script distinctions. What remains is an ASCII string that machines can parse but that no longer speaks with its original voice.

The Unicode restoration recovers what ASCII flattened. Aša restores original diacritics and script distinctions, returning the name to its original written dignity. The domain encodes to Punycode, but the browser displays the truth.

Original Script Provenance

How Aša travels from ancient script to scholarly transliteration

From original to transliteration

  1. Avestan letters A (𐬀), Š (𐬴), and A (𐬀) spell aša
  2. The caron in the Latin transliteration marks the postalveolar fricative /ʃ/
  3. Central to the Gathic hymns (Yasna), where aša is paired with Vohu Manah and the Amesha Spentas
