The Myth at Work

The Nocturnal Battle in the Duat. In the Amduat, the Book of Gates, and other New Kingdom underworld books, Ra's barque sails through twelve hours of night. In the seventh hour Apophis waits, a vast serpent coiled in the river of the Duat. — hold that image; the industries below are where it goes to work.

The Book of Overthrowing Apophis. Preserved on papyri and temple walls, this liturgical text instructs priests to make wax images of Apophis, pierce them with knives, burn them, trample them, and recite spells that sever his vertebrae and scatter his body. The ritual was performed daily in major temples to ensure that the sun would rise. — hold that image; the industries below are where it goes to work.
