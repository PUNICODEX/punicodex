The Authentic Name

Why Asía.com is the correct form

Original Script

Ἀσία

The name in its original Greek form. Asía (Ἀσία) is attested as personified continent of asia — “The eastern continent (possibly from Assuwa)”. Its acute accents carry the full phonetic and orthographic weight of the source tradition.

ASCII Constraint

asia

Reduced to plain asia, the name loses everything that made it specific: acute accents. What remains is an ASCII string that machines can parse but that no longer speaks with its original voice.

Unicode Restoration

Asía

The Unicode restoration recovers what ASCII flattened. Asía restores acute accents, returning the name to its original written dignity. The domain encodes to Punycode, but the browser displays the truth.

Punycode Encoding
Asía.com → xn--asa-sma.com

The non-ASCII characters in Asía are encoded while the ASCII remains visible. To the DNS, it is Punycode. To humanity, it is Asía.

Mythology

Stories of Asía

Asía belongs to the greek-location tradition as personified continent of asia.

The eastern continent (possibly from Assuwa)

Etymologically, it derives from *Aswia (“east, sunrise”), a root that shaped cult titles, hymns, and ritual addresses across centuries.

The myths surrounding this figure established its authority in ritual, art, and literature — and continue to surface in later religious and literary traditions.
