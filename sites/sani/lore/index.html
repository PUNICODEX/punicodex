The Authentic Name

Unicode restoration and ASCII comparison

Original Script

शनि

The name in its original Sanskrit form. Śani (शनि) is attested in the source tradition — “"The Slow One" — Saturn as the lord of karma, justice, discipline, and time”. Its original diacritics and script distinctions carry the full phonetic and orthographic weight of the source tradition.

ASCII Constraint

sani

Reduced to plain sani, the name loses everything that made it specific: original diacritics and script distinctions. What remains is an ASCII string that machines can parse but that no longer speaks with its original voice.

Unicode Restoration

Śani

The Unicode restoration recovers what ASCII flattened. Śani restores original diacritics and script distinctions, returning the name to its original written dignity. The domain encodes to Punycode, but the browser displays the truth.

Punycode Encoding
Śani.com → xn--ani-bza.com

The non-ASCII characters in Śani are encoded while the ASCII remains visible. To the DNS, it is Punycode. To humanity, it is Śani.

The Accent / Script Rule

Sanskrit śani — "the slow one" — begins with the palatal ś (IAST's acute-accented s) and the short a and i. The restoration keeps the ś that English writes "sh": Śani, the slow planet, whose very name is a warning about haste. The ś is the palatal sibilant, made at the roof of the mouth farther forward than sh — the sound English approximates but never quite keeps.

The Slow Reckoning

Saturn, Karma, Justice, and the Seven-and-a-Half Years

Śani is the most feared of the nine planets: Saturn as the auditor of karma, whose slow passage through the zodiac delivers every deed's return — never early, never late, and never, ever argued with. India loves him exactly as much as it fears him, which is to say, enormously.

Sade Sati

The seven-and-a-half years of his transit over your moon — India's most-dreaded astrological season.

The Auditor

Karma's bookkeeper: no bribe, no appeal, no clerical error — only the account.

Son of the Sun

Sūrya's estranged son — the shadow the light itself begot.

The Crow

His vāhana: the dark bird that sees what others will not look at.

Sacred Symbols

The crow The dark mount — witness of the unavoidable
The iron His metal — hard, cold, unglamorous, indispensable
The slow orbit Twenty-nine-and-a-half years — time as the measure of justice
06

Mythology

Stories of Śani

Śani's myths are about the impossibility of escaping consequence — even for gods, and especially for kings.

The Birth

The Shadow of the Sun

Born of Sūrya and Chhāyā — "Shadow," the substitute-wife the sun's true wife left in her place — Śani was the son the light refused to look at. His gaze fell so baleful that his own father's skin darkened at the meeting. The Purāṇas make the estrangement permanent: the planet of justice is the sun's shadow-child, and no amount of brilliance exempts the family from audit.

The Gaze

Even the Gods Are Judged

His stories form a single syllabus: when Śani's gaze fell on Śiva, the great god was made to wander as a beggar; on Rāma's line, even the righteous king's fortune bowed. The Hanumān legend adds the one counter: when Śani pressed Hanumān, the mighty one crushed him instead — and Śani, begging release, vowed never to afflict Hanumān's devotees. Devotion freely given is the only shield Śani honors — the one god who bows to bhakti.

The Season

Sade Sati

The living myth is the calendar: when Saturn transits the sign before, of, and after your natal moon — seven and a half years — the astrologers warn of Sade Sati, the season of testing. Careers stall, elders ail, patience thins — and the remedy prescribed is the same everywhere: sesame oil, iron, crows fed, and Śani's Saturday fast. The most-followed planetary observance in India is his.

Extended Lore

Śani is the god of "later." Every tradition has a debt-collector, but his genius is slowness: the audit arrives when the deeds are half-forgotten, itemized to the day. He asks the only question that matters at midlife: not what did you build, but what did you balance — and are you ready for the season when the slow planet comes to check?
