01

The Authentic Name

Unicode restoration and ASCII comparison

Original Script

बोधिसत्त्व

The name in its original Buddhist form. Bodhisattva (बोधिसत्त्व) is attested in the source tradition — “'Awakening-being' — one who vows Buddhahood for the sake of all beings”. Its original diacritics and script distinctions carry the full phonetic and orthographic weight of the source tradition.

ASCII Constraint

boddhisattva

The plain boddhisattva form is identical to the Unicode restoration. Because this name is already written in Latin letters, no diacritics, stress, or script information were lost — only capitalization differs.

Unicode Restoration

Bodhisattva

The Unicode restoration does not need to recover lost marks for Bodhisattva. Its value is canonical spelling and consistent cataloguing, not the reconstruction of erased orthography. The domain is readable as-is to both DNS and humanity.

Punycode Encoding
Bodhisattva.com → bodhisattva.com

The non-ASCII characters in Bodhisattva are encoded while the ASCII remains visible. To the DNS, it is Punycode. To humanity, it is Bodhisattva.

Mythology

Stories of Bodhisattva

The stories of Bodhisattva place enlightenment being, compassion at the center of a living mythological world.

Myth

Origins

Bodhisattva appears in buddhist tradition as enlightenment being, compassion. The name's meaning, "'Awakening-being' — one who vows Buddhahood for the sake of all beings", shapes the narratives told about this figure.

Cult

Worship and Memory

Devotees and later tradition-keepers preserved Bodhisattva in ritual, text, and iconography, ensuring the name survived into the modern scholarly record.
