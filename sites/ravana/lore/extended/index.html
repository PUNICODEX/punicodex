Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Rāvaṇa, Ten-Headed King, Lanka, and Unicode restoration

01How do you pronounce Rāvaṇa?

In reconstructed pronunciation, Rāvaṇa is /ravana/ — approximately 'RAVANA' — pronounce the restored form with attention to the preserved diacritics and length..

02What does Rāvaṇa mean?

Rāvaṇa means The ruler of Laṅkā and chief of the Rākṣasas, whose destruction by Rāmacandra forms the subject of the Rāmāyaṇa in the sanskrit tradition.

03What are the symbols of Rāvaṇa?

Rāvaṇa is associated with Restored Name (The Unicode form Rāvaṇa carries the original marks of the Sanskrit tradition.), Source Script (Preserved through scholarly transliteration and canonical sources.), Domain (Ten-Headed King, Lanka), Etymology (The ruler of Laṅkā and chief of the Rākṣasas, whose destruction by Rāmacandra forms the subject of the Rāmāyaṇa).

04Why restore Rāvaṇa in Unicode?

Plain ASCII ravana strips the stress, length, and script that make the name specific. Unicode restoration returns the name to its original written dignity.
