Allard Pierson Museum Thoth column 7673 — Moongod Toth. The wise god Thoth has, like the moon, two faces: with a full moon the round head of a baboon, during the waxing period that of an ibis. Here the baboon is seated on a column with the ibis below. Of Thoth himself all we see is his hand on the face of the moon, the rest of the statue is lacking. In Roman-Egypt Thoth is equal to the god Mercurius. Marble, Egypt (?), c. 100-150 AD (inv. 7946) (Dosseman, CC BY-SA 4.0)
