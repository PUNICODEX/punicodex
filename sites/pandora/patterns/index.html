Why this map exists

Advertisers and patrons do not buy space on a single temple — they join a constellation. Every industry arc above lists the sister temples that share its current, so a placement can be chosen by the whole pattern it belongs to. Researchers and the PUNICODEX API consume the same map as structured data.
