Vasubandhu is abhidharma, yogacara. The name means "The celebrated Buddhist scholar — author of the Abhidharmakośa, co-founder of the Yogācāra school" and belongs to the buddhist tradition.

Vasubandhu remains a touchstone for understanding buddhist religion, art, and identity.
