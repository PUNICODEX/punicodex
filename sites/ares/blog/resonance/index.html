The Cycle, Read Forward

Defense, Military & Security — through Struck by Diomedes

In Iliad 5 (855–863), the Greek hero Diomedes, aided by Athena, wounds Árēs, Athena guiding his spear. The god roars like ten thousand warriors and flees to Olympus to complain.

The atlas states the seat in one line: War in its rawest form — battle fury, bloodlust, and the chaos of combat; the Greeks’ own name for the fight. The story above is the long version of the same sentence. That is the cycle working: story becomes duty, duty becomes trade.

Aviation & Aerospace — through Caught with Aphrodítē

Hêphaistos, discovering his wife Aphrodítē's affair with Árēs, forged an unbreakable net and trapped the lovers in bed. The other gods gathered to laugh.

The atlas states the seat in one line: NASA's Ares launchers were built to carry crews Moonward under the war god's name. The story above is the long version of the same sentence. The trade thinks it is optimizing a process. It is re-enacting a charter.

Gaming, Fantasy & Entertainment IP — through Ares and the Dragon of Thebes

The spring later called the Ismenian at Thebes was guarded by a dragon said to be Árēs's own offspring. When it killed the men Kadmos sent for water, Kadmos slew the dragon and, on Athena's advice, sowed its teeth, from which sprang the Spartoi, the armed ancestors of the Theban nobility.

The atlas states the seat in one line: God of War built a franchise on his title and made him its first final boss. The story above is the long version of the same sentence. Industries do not invent their archetypes; they inherit them and call them best practice.

Legal, Justice & Compliance — through Father of Harmonia

By Aphrodítē, Árēs fathered Harmonia, whose name means 'agreement' — the peace that follows war. Their union produced also Phobos and Deimos, Fear and Terror, who attend their father into battle.

The atlas states the seat in one line: The Areopagus — his hill — judged Athenian homicide for a millennium; myth makes its first defendant Árēs himself. The story above is the long version of the same sentence. The trade thinks it is optimizing a process. It is re-enacting a charter.

The Theme That Runs the Cycle

Árēs is the only major Olympian whom other gods routinely humiliate. His myths emphasize that war without wisdom is self-defeating.
