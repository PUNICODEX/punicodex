Cultural Significance

From ancient cult to modern Unicode

Ancient Domain

Kotoshironushi is the son of Ōkuninushi who, according to the Kojiki, surrendered the central land of reed plains to the heavenly descendants of Amaterasu. A god of oracles and fishing, he withdrew to the sea and became identified in later tradition with the fortune god Ebisu.

Kotoshironushi in Later Traditions

Kotoshironushi is a nodal figure connecting the land-transfer myth, oracle cults, and the later Ebisu tradition. He represents the old order's willing surrender and the sea's continuing bounty. His absorption into Ebisu is one of the clearest cases of mythological condensation in Japanese folk religion.

Modern Legacy

Kotoshironushi is rarely worshipped independently today, but his legacy lives in Ebisu and in the story of how the imperial line gained the land without total war. He is remembered as the son who advised surrender for the greater good.

Unicode Restoration as Cultural Act

Restoring Kotoshironushi in a domain name is more than orthographic accuracy. It is a statement that the internet should recognize the full range of human writing — not only the ASCII keyboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Kotoshironushi, Oracles, Fishing, and Unicode restoration

01How do you pronounce Kotoshironushi?

In reconstructed pronunciation, Kotoshironushi is /ko.to.ɕi.ɾo.nɯ.ɕi/ — approximately 'KOH-toh-shee-roh-noo-shee' — the 'r' is a light tap, and the final vowels are soft..

02What does Kotoshironushi mean?

Kotoshironushi means Lord who substitutes in the japanese tradition.

03What are the symbols of Kotoshironushi?

Kotoshironushi is associated with Fishing rod (His attribute as a sea god), Sea bream (The auspicious fish associated with Ebisu and maritime fortune), Oracle implements (Tools of divination used in land-transfer negotiations), Bow (Symbol of the martial power that persuaded the earthly kami to submit).

04Why restore Kotoshironushi in Unicode?

Plain ASCII kotoshironushi strips the stress, length, and script that make the name specific. Unicode restoration returns the name to its original written dignity.

05What is the most important myth about Kotoshironushi?

When Amaterasu sent her grandson Ninigi to rule the earth, the earthly kami Ōkuninushi resisted. After negotiations and divine pressure, Ōkuninushi asked his son Kotoshironushi for advice. Kotoshironushi declared that the land should be surrendered to the heavenly descendants, and he withdrew to the sea. His voluntary submission made the peaceful transfer possible.
