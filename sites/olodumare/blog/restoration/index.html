The Olódùmarè file: from olodumare to the temple it became

The domain you're reading about exists because someone refused to let Olódùmarè stay olodumare.

Olódùmarè's mythology is theology: it concerns the origin of the world, the distribution of destiny, and the hierarchy between the high god and the orishas. Olódùmarè created the universe and then sent the orishas down to finish it. Ọbatalá shaped human bodies from clay, and Olódùmarè himself breathed life into them.

That is the figure this file is about — and the temple now standing at its restored name.
