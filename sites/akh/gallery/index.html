Sacred Images

Archaeological finds from 3000 years of Egyptian afterlife theology

Weighing of the Heart

The Weighing of the Heart

Book of the Dead of Hunefer, c. 1275 BCE — Anubis kneels beside the scales as the heart is weighed against the feather of Ma'at.

Anubis Shrine

The Anubis Shrine of Tutankhamun

Tomb of Tutankhamun (KV62), c. 1323 BCE — A life-sized recumbent Anubis jackal, carved from wood and gilded, guarding the canopic shrine.

Opening of the Mouth

The Opening of the Mouth Ceremony

Tomb of Seti I (KV17), c. 1279 BCE — The sem priest, wearing a leopard skin and Anubis mask, restores the senses to the mummy.

Field of Reeds

The Field of Reeds

Tomb of Sennedjem (TT1), c. 1279 BCE — The Egyptian paradise, where the justified akh lives forever among abundant wheat and clear water.

Canopic Jars

Canopic Equipment of Tutankhamun

Tomb of Tutankhamun, c. 1323 BCE — Four canopic jars, each with a lid shaped like the king's face, protected by four goddesses.

Pyramid Texts

Pyramid Texts of Unas

Pyramid of Unas, c. 2400 BCE — The oldest religious texts in the world, inscribed in blue hieroglyphs on the walls of the royal tomb.
