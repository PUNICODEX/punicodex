Ragnarǫk through the eyes of sculptors, painters, and craftsmen across the ages

Ragnarök — Johannes Gehrts. The final battle between the gods of Asgard and Loki's offspring: fire, flood, and the end of worlds.

After Ragnarök — Emil Doepler, c. 1905. The world reborn from the ashes, green shoots rising over the sea.

Surtr — John Charles Dollman, 1909. The fire giant of Muspellheim raises his blazing sword to set the world aflame.

The Death of Odin and Freyr — Emil Doepler. Odin falls to Fenrir's jaws; Freyr meets Surtr's flame. Two gods, two deaths, one doom.

The Binding of Fenrir — George Wright. The wolf who will one day break free and swallow the All-Father is chained by craft and courage.
