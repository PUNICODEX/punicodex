The Myth at Work

Born in a Cave on Mount Cyllene. Hermês was born to Zeús and the nymph Maia in a remote Arcadian cave. By midday of his first day he had invented the lyre from a tortoise shell and slipped out to steal Apóllōn's cattle. — that is not just a story. It is a business model, four thousand years early.

The Lyre for the Herd. Zeús ordered Hermês to return the cattle. Hermês played the lyre he had invented, and Apóllōn was so enchanted that he traded his whole herd for the instrument. — remember the shape of that act. You will see it again, wearing a logo.
