- **Wind** = Fuel costs, carbon regulations, alternative propulsion (sails returning to container ships)
- **Current** = Route optimisation, Suez vs Panama vs Cape of Good Hope, just-in-time scheduling
- **Depth** = Draft limitations, port infrastructure, the Panama Canal expansion that reshaped global logistics

When Poseidon struck the earth with his trident, he created horses — the fastest land transport of the ancient world. When he struck the sea, he created **storms** — the force that could destroy a fleet or deliver it to victory. The trident is not a weapon. It is a **lever of economic power**.

Njord's most telling attribute is his **foot-washing**. Sailors washed his feet before voyages as a ritual of preparation and respect. This is not superstition.
