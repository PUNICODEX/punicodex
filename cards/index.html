The First Restoration Set · FR1

Mythic Cards

Every temple of the Pantheon, restored as a collectible card — 1,156 cards across 25 pantheons: 271 legendaries, 232 original-script chase foils, and the full depth of the lexicon beneath them.

Restoring the set…

Every card is a temple

Each card is generated from the same canonical lexicon as the temples — the stats from scholarly significance, the rarities from the tier system, the art from each temple's own materials. Open a card to step into its temple, or take your collection into Mythic Duel and battle with it.
