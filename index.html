Station IV · The Beginning

WHY DO THE GODS
LIVE IN ASCII?

Zeús became “Zeus.” Apóllōn became “Apollo.” The pitch accents vanished; the long vowels collapsed — centuries of philological precision erased by a keyboard layout built for English teleprinters. PUNICODEX reverses that erasure: original spelling, attested sources, the rightful Unicode form — one character at a time.
