# PuniCodex — Pre-Flagship Entries (the promotion queue)

Generated from the canonical lexicon (type/js/lexicon.js) against the flagship archetype list
(js/archetypes-v2.js). These 435 entries are restored names that do NOT yet have a
hand-crafted flagship temple — their pages are the generated base temples. They are the promotion
queue: each one you prepare materials for can become a flagship via `node scripts/promote-to-flagship.js <id>`.

Sorted by pantheon (largest queue first), entries alphabetical within each tradition.
