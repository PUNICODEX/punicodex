# PÚNYCODEX Scholarly Edition — Onboarding Guide

## For Students

1. **Log in** with your university email at `/scholars/login/index.html`.
2. Choose a flagship temple and open its **Scholars** tab.
3. Click **Propose Edit** on any section.
4. Write scholarly content, cite sources, and submit.
5. Wait for a faculty reviewer to approve your edit.

## For Faculty Reviewers

1. Log in with your university email.
2. Visit `/scholars/review/index.html`.
3. Review pending edits from your institution.
4. Approve, reject, or request revision with a comment.

## For Institution Admins

1. Log in and visit `/scholars/institution/index.html`.
2. Invite members, assign roles, and monitor contributions.
3. Download impact reports for your department.

## For PÚNYCODEX Curators

1. Visit `/scholars/admin/index.html`.
2. Monitor stats, freeze temples in disputes, and override reviews when necessary.

## Content Standards

- Cite sources from the hierarchy in `ACCURACY.md`.
- Avoid original research and religious advocacy.
- Use the markdown-like editor for paragraphs; sources are listed separately.

## Support

Contact the PÚNYCODEX curator team for disputes, partnership inquiries, or technical issues.
