2Suggestions & templesNames›

The keyboard carries the whole PuniCodex lexicon. Type any god, goddess or mythological name in ordinary letters, anywhere — Messages, Notes, any app — and the suggestion bar offers the scholarly Unicode spelling.

Tap a chip

to replace what you typed with the full restoration: apollon → Apóllōn, hera → Hēra, zeus → Zeús.

Long-press a chip

for the scholarly variant sheet — full restoration, circumflex form, macron-only (LSJ convention) and plain ASCII, each with its punycode domain.

Temple card

every suggestion links to its flagship temple on punicodex.com — meaning, pantheon, sources, and the god's live Unicode domain. The keyboard is the doorway; the temple is the home.

3Long-press, everywhereVariants›

Every key on both panels hides a curated variant sheet behind a long-press. Press and hold, slide to the character you want, release.

Letters

5–10 scholarly forms per letter, curated separately for lower and upper case — hold a for á à â ä ã å ā ă ǎ, h for ĥ ḥ ḫ, t for þ, s for ß.

Digits

Roman numerals: hold 1 for Ⅰ, Ⅱ, Ⅲ…

Symbols

hold $ for ¢ € £ ¥ ₹, " for “ ” « », - for – — −, < for ≤ ≪ ⟨ — across the whole symbol panel.

Your additions

any variant you pin via the Ω directory joins the sheet in orange, ahead of the defaults. Chapter 5.

4The Ω directory7,728 chars›

Tap Ω — it lights purple — to open a complete Unicode workbench: 7,728 characters in 49 categories, fully offline. Greek, Coptic, Glagolitic, runes, Egyptian hieroglyphs, cuneiform, Linear B, Gothic, Ogham, Phoenician, Baybayin, math, arrows, technical signs, braille, alchemical sigils, dominoes, enclosed alphanumerics, combining marks and more. Every cell shows the glyph, its name and its U+ code.

Insert

tap a character: it is typed into your field and the directory closes. The keyboard stays live while the directory is open.

Categories & search

chips filter instantly; the directory remembers your last category between sessions. Search matches names, keywords and U+ codes.

Multi-select

the toggle (or long-press Ω) queues characters into a string — build ᛟᚱᚦᛟᚱ piece by piece, then Copy, Insert or Clear.

Move, resize, transparency

drag the PuniCodex Directory header; − / + resize; ◐ toggles see-through; ▼ collapses the key rows. All remembered.

Close

tap Ω again — it glows purple while the directory is open.

5Make it yoursPersonal›
Pin any character to any key

in the Ω directory, long-press the character you want — the keyboard glows orange and a banner shows what is pending. Tap the target key; letters pin to both cases. Tap ✕ to cancel.

Remove a custom variant

long-press the key, then long-press the orange variant in its sheet and confirm Delete. Built-in defaults are permanent.

Customise the All view

pin up to 6 categories (and their order) to the front of All in the All starts with box. Your arrangement is saved.

Everything persists

pins, deletions, All arrangement, directory size, opacity and last category all survive restarts and updates.
