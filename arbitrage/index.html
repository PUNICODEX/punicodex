DOMAIN ARBITRAGE

Acquire Unicode domains through PuniCodex. You find the name — we quote it in writing, acquire it through an ICANN-accredited registrar, configure the DNS, and hand you the keys.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Find an available name. Search the lexicon in Available mode — every result shows the name's current registration status across five registrars.
  2. Apply below. We answer with a written quote: the registrar's public price plus a PuniCodex acquisition & configuration fee, both itemized and disclosed before anything is charged.
  3. We acquire, configure, and hand over. DNS configured, control transferred to you. Your domain is then crawled and indexed by the PuniCodex search engine free of charge, forever — with an Authenticity watch kept on lookalikes.

STRAIGHT TERMS

Not a Registrar

PuniCodex is not a registrar and does not sell registrations. Every acquisition is fulfilled through an ICANN-accredited registrar on your behalf.

Disclosed Pricing

You pay the registrar's public price plus our acquisition & configuration service fee. Both appear in the written quote you approve before anything is charged — no hidden margin, no surprise line items.

No Availability Guarantee

Anyone can register an available name at any moment. We cannot guarantee a name remains available until the acquisition completes — if it is taken first, you pay nothing.

ICANN Transfers

Ownership transfers follow ICANN transfer policy, including the standard 60-day transfer lock after registration. We walk the paperwork through with you until the name sits in your own registrar account.
