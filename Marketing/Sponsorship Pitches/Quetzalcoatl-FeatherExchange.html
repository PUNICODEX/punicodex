01 — THE RESONANCE
Why Quetzalcōātl is your temple
◆  No god in the pantheon carries your product in his name.
His name is a compound: quetzal, the resplendent bird of the cloud forest, and cóātl, serpent — the Feathered Serpent. Of 271 restored temples, this is the one whose very name is a feather. You are not adjacent to this archetype; you are named from the same plumage.
◆  Feathers were treasure beyond gold — and an empire ran on their exchange.
In Mesoamerica, quetzal plumes were the most precious commodity in existence, gathered as tribute from conquered provinces and traded across the Triple Alliance. The Florentine Codex records feather as currency, as regalia, as the raw material of the imperial amanteca feather-workers' guild. Nine centuries before your domain was registered, there was already a feather exchange — and this god presided over it.
◆  The god of makers, not takers.
Quetzalcōātl is the artisan-god of Mesoamerica: teacher of weaving, metalwork, and feather-craft to humankind; patron of the guilds; the civilizer rather than the conqueror. A business built on careful sourcing, honest grading, and fair exchange speaks this god's native language — commerce as a craft, not an extraction.
◆  Trade winds are his province.
In his Ehēcatl aspect he is the wind itself — the wind that sweeps the road before the rain, the current that carried canoes and, later, galleons. Every exchange begins with something arriving from somewhere. The Morning Star, his celestial aspect, is the oldest navigational fix traders ever steered by.
◆  The temple's pattern map already includes you.
Our Industry Patterns map — the same structured data consumed by researchers and our API — aligns this temple with Education, Research & Knowledge ("Mesoamerica's teacher-god"), Space, Astronomy & Celestial Science (the Morning Star aspect), and Wind & Atmospheric Energy (the Ehēcatl aspect): curation, provenance, and movement — the three disciplines of any serious exchange.
