The Unicode Herald — Q3 2026

New This Quarter

Screen guide, pantheon pages, and domainless flagships

Per-Pantheon Landing Pages

Twenty-five dedicated pages now open the major traditions — /greek/, /norse/, /egyptian/, /sanskrit/, /japanese/, and twenty more. Each page tells the cultural story of the pantheon, links to its sacred texts where they exist, and displays every flagship temple in that tradition as a browsable grid. Built for discovery and for search engines alike.

The Screen Guide

A new /screen/ guide maps films, series, and games that feature mythological characters back to their temples. From Percy Jackson and the Olympians to Princess Mononoke, God of War Ragnarök to Record of Ragnarok, each entry explains what the production got right, where it took liberties, and which temples to visit for the scholarly source.

Domainless Flagships

Many names central to world mythology — Susanō, Helénē, Rāvaṇa, Óneiros — already own their .com homes. Many others do not. A new class of flagship temple now exists for unregisterable or taken ASCII names, created with the same scholarly depth as owned temples but without claiming a domain. These expand the pantheon's coverage without diluting its integrity.
