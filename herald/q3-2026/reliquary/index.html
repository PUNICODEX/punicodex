The Reliquary

Print-on-demand and the physical pantheon

The Reliquary is the print-on-demand arm of the project. Every flagship temple now has thirteen product types — tee, hoodie, crewneck, cap, mug, tumbler, tote, phone case, notebook, sticker, pin, print, and canvas — plus an eight-item house line. Products are produced on demand through Printful, keeping inventory risk low while offering physical artifacts tied to the temples.

Next quarter the store pipeline will be connected to Google Merchant Center and strengthened with mockup automation, variant pricing, and marketing collateral so the temples can reach buyers beyond the site itself.
