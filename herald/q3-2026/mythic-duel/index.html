Mythic Duel & Cards

The game under construction

The First Restoration card set and the Mythic Duel engine remain under active construction. Every flagship temple prints common, rare, legendary, and mythic editions; domainless flagships enter as archive printings and can climb on promotion. The deterministic duel engine uses seeded RNG so matches are replayable and fair.

In August the card work took a deliberate back seat so the pantheon expansion could reach 542 temples. With that milestone reached, the next quarter returns focus to pack logic, ink economy, card renderer polish, and mobile play.
