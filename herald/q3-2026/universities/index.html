The Unicode Herald — Q3 2026

University Collaboration

Removing the fee, opening the door

The Scholarly Edition is edited under university sponsorship. For Q4 2026 PuniCodex is moving away from the $999-per-year institutional fee and toward a barter model: universities provide curation, student contributors, and a cross-link from their humanities or history department; PuniCodex displays their logo on the academic collaborators strip and gives their students free access to technological, scholarly, and utility features.

The goal is to align the project's growth with free educational value. Student contributors gain portfolio work in digital humanities, sponsors gain a vetted creative pipeline, and the pantheon gains peer-reviewed depth.
